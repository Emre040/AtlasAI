**Limitations**

- Single cell RNA sequencing data reflect relative normalized counts per million (nCPM) from dissociated human cell types and do not represent absolute transcript copy numbers per cell or in vivo protein levels.
- Prognostic associations from TCGA clinical cohorts reflect statistical survival correlations rather than demonstrated causal mechanisms of tumor progression or response.
