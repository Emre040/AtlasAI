**Limitations**

- Prognostic associations from pathology survival analyses in the Human Protein Atlas reflect statistical associations with patient survival and do not demonstrate causal tumor suppression or oncogenesis.
- Cohorts with 0 validated prognostic genes (Prostate adenocarcinoma, Stomach adenocarcinoma, Testicular germ cell tumor, and Thyroid carcinoma) had no genes passing the validated prognostic significance criteria in the TCGA pathology cohorts table.
- Tissue enrichment is defined according to the Human Protein Atlas RNA tissue specificity category 'Tissue enriched' (at least fourfold higher mRNA level in a particular tissue compared to any other tissue). Other specificity categories such as group enriched or tissue enhanced were not included.
