**Limitations**

- Tissue enrichment categories reflect relative expression specificity (at least 4-fold higher mRNA level in a particular tissue compared to all other tissues) in consensus RNA data and do not reflect absolute molar abundance or protein copy numbers.
- Secretome annotations and FDA approved drug target annotations reflect curated database assignments and cataloged protein classes from the Human Protein Atlas, not active pharmacological modulation or functional secretion validated in every physiological tissue state.
