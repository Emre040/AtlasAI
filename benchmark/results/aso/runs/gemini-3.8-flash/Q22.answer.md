**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

The current release of the Human Protein Atlas is version 25.1 [1]. In version 25.1, the atlas added Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data to the Single Cell resource, featuring cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 different cell types representing 14 different tissues, matched with Single Cell Type data to enable side-by-side comparison of RNA and protein data [1]. Additionally, quantitative MS-based proteomics profiles for bulk tissue samples across 20 different tissue types overlapping with the DVP samples were integrated into the Tissue resource, supplying protein profiles for over 12,700 genes [1]. For the version 25 release line, the atlas covers over 27,800 antibodies targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes, which represents approximately 88% of human protein-coding genes [2].
