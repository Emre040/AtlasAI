**Limitations**

- Consensus PPI interactions are restricted to supported edges in consensus databases (such as BioGRID, IntAct, BioPlex); absence of an edge indicates lack of consensus PPI record rather than proven lack of physical interaction in vivo.
- Subcellular main locations are missing for several proteins in HPA immunofluorescence annotations, which represents unrecorded data rather than absence of subcellular localization.
- RNA nTPM consensus values reflect relative steady-state transcript abundance and do not represent absolute molecular copy numbers or direct protein concentrations.
