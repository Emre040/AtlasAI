**Findings**

- Six genes annotated as secreted to blood are tissue enriched in pancreas at the RNA level: PPY, GCG, IAPP, GPHA2, INS, and CGB1. In liver consensus transcriptomics (rna_tissue_consensus), PPY (0.0 nTPM), GCG (0.0 nTPM), and CGB1 (0.0 nTPM) have an expression value of 0.0 nTPM, while GPHA2 has 0.6 nTPM (which is below the standard HPA RNA detection cutoff of nTPM < 1). Depending on whether 'not detected' is defined strictly as nTPM = 0.0 or under the standard HPA cutoff of nTPM < 1: under the strict 0.0 nTPM threshold, there are 3 genes (PPY, GCG, CGB1); under the nTPM < 1 detection threshold, there are 4 genes (PPY, GCG, GPHA2, CGB1). In contrast, IAPP (2.2 nTPM) and INS (1.9 nTPM) are detected in liver with nTPM >= 1. **Limitations**

- Whether 'not detected' corresponds to an exact value of 0.0 nTPM or the standard Human Protein Atlas transcript detection threshold of nTPM < 1 affects the inclusion of GPHA2 (0.6 nTPM in liver). Both interpretations are reported.
- Protein-level detection (e.g., IHC in liver) was not evaluated as liver IHC staining profiles were not retrieved; detection is assessed at the consensus RNA level (nTPM).
