**Limitations**

- A blank or missing MS intensity record represents an unmeasured gene or a level below mass spectrometry detection limits in the assay; it cannot be treated as a zero or proof of biological absence.
- True median protein abundance across all 20 genes cannot be computed because 16 genes lack colon MS intensity records, and missing values cannot be substituted with numerical zeros.
- Fraction of biologically absent proteins cannot be calculated from mass-spectrometry non-detection, as absence of detection does not demonstrate biological absence.
