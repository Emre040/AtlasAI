**Limitations**

- The Human Protein Atlas provides relative measurements: normalized transcript abundance (nTPM in rna_tissue_consensus.tsv), relative mass-spectrometry signal intensity (Intensity in ms_tissue.tsv), and relative differential protein abundance (log2 fold change in cancer_cptac.tsv). Absolute copy numbers (molecules per cell) and absolute protein-to-RNA molecule counts cannot be calculated from these relative levels without external spike-in standards, cell volume metrics, or absolute calibration curves.
- MS intensity and RNA nTPM reflect bulk lung tissue profiling, whereas CPTAC represents differential abundance between lung adenocarcinoma and paired normal tissue. Relative changes cannot be combined cross-assay into absolute molecular inventories per cell.
