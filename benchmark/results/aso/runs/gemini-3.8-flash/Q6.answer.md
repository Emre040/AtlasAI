**Findings**

- There are 123 interaction partners of TP53 recorded in the Human Protein Atlas consensus interaction dataset. (evidence: a23 row 0: count=123)
- Among the 114 TP53 interaction partners with recorded subcellular location data (9 partner genes lack records), the most frequent main subcellular locations are Nucleoplasm (56 genes), Cytosol (14 genes), Cytosol;Nucleoplasm (7 genes), Nuclear bodies;Nucleoplasm (5 genes), Cytosol;Plasma membrane (4 genes), Nucleoli;Nucleoplasm (4 genes), Nuclear bodies (3 genes), and Mitochondria (3 genes). (evidence: a14 row 0: count=56, location_item=Nucleoplasm; row 1: count=14, location_item=Cytosol; row 2: count=7, location_item=Cytosol;Nucleoplasm; row 3: count=5, location_item=Nuclear bodies;Nucleoplasm; row 4: count=4, location_item=Cytosol;Plasma membrane; row 5: count=4, location_item=Nucleoli;Nucleoplasm; row 6: count=3, location_item=Nuclear bodies; row 7: count=3, location_item=Mitochondria | a21 row 0: recorded_Main location=114, missing_Main location=9)
- Exactly 86 of the 114 TP53 interaction partners with recorded main subcellular locations include the nucleus (such as Nucleoplasm, Nuclear bodies, Nucleoli, or Nuclear membrane), out of the total 123 interaction partners. (evidence: a22 row 0: count=86 | a21 row 0: count=123, recorded_Main location=114)
- A total of 47 distinct TP53 interaction partners have a validated prognostic association across TCGA cancer cohorts (spanning 57 gene-cohort validated associations: 19 favorable associations and 38 unfavorable associations). (evidence: a24 row 0: count=57, distinct_gene=47)

**Limitations**

- Subcellular location data were available for 114 of the 123 TP53 interaction partners; 9 genes had missing records in subcellular_location.tsv.
- Prognostic associations in TCGA cohorts are correlative relationships between mRNA expression levels and patient survival outcomes, not evidence of direct causal mechanisms.
