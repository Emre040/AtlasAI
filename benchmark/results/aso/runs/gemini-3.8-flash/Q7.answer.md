**Limitations**

- Validated prognostic annotations are available only for a subset of cancer cohorts in HPA; cohorts without validated entries appear as blanks rather than confirmed absences or biological zeroes.
- The prognostic classifications reflect statistical associations with patient survival and do not establish causal biological mechanisms or direct clinical therapeutic efficacy.
