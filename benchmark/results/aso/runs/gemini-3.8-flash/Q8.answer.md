**Limitations**

- Blood concentrations in the Human Protein Atlas reflect pooled/cohort measurements from distinct experimental platforms and published studies; discrepancies between immunoassay and mass spectrometry may arise from differences in epitope specificity, cross-reactivity, proteoform/cleavage-state detection, lower limit of quantification, or standardization rather than absolute true biological variation.
- The initial query cohort was retrieved via secretome annotation categories covering secreted proteins; any non-secreted proteins that might possess blood concentration records in HPA were not included in the secretome-annotated search space.
