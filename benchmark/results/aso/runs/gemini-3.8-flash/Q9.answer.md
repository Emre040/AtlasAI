**Limitations**

- Regional transcriptomic measurements are recorded in normalized transcripts per million (nTPM) from consensus brain regional RNA-seq datasets; they represent relative normalized RNA levels across anatomical structures rather than absolute molecular concentrations or cell-type-specific resolution.
- The Spearman correlation is evaluated across 13 defined anatomical brain regions and reflects general concordances in macroscopic expression patterns, not functional co-regulation or causal interactions.
