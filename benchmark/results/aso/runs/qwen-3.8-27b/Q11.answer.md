**Limitations**

- The result reflects the current Human Protein Atlas annotation: 'validated unfavourable' requires a published validation study in the LHC TCGA cohort, and 'tissue enriched' requires the gene's RNA expression to be significantly higher in liver than in all other tissues. A gene could be prognostically relevant in liver cancer without being liver-specific at the RNA level (e.g., proliferation or immune genes upregulated in the tumour microenvironment).
- The intersection is computed on gene symbols as recorded in the atlas; any discrepancy in symbol usage between the two annotation pipelines would not be detected here.
- This finding does not exclude biological overlap at the protein level or in other tissues; it is specific to the two atlas categories as defined.
