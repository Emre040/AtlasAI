**Limitations**

- Only 8 of the 12 requested TCGA cohorts have validated prognostic gene annotations in the Human Protein Atlas. Stomach adenocarcinoma, prostate adenocarcinoma, thyroid carcinoma, and testicular germ cell tumor are absent from the atlas's prognostic annotation and therefore cannot be reported.
- The tissue-enrichment set for colon was retrieved under the atlas category 'Intestine' rather than a colon-specific category; the overlap count for colon (0) may be affected if the atlas distinguishes colon from intestine in a way not captured here.
- The very large favourable count for kidney (1,639) relative to other cohorts may reflect a broader annotation scope or different curation criteria in the atlas for that cohort; the proportion tissue-enriched (1%) is correspondingly low.
- Tissue enrichment is defined by the atlas as ≥4-fold higher mRNA in the tissue of origin versus any other tissue; this is a bulk-tissue measure and does not capture cell-type-specific expression within the tissue.
