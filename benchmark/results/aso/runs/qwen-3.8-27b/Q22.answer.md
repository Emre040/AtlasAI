**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

The current release of the Human Protein Atlas is version 25.1 [1]. In this release, the new data added were Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data in the Single Cell resource, giving cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 different cell types representing 14 different tissues, with selected Single Cell Type RNA data matched to the DVP samples for side-by-side RNA-to-protein comparison, and MS-based quantitative proteomics profiles for bulk tissue samples in the Tissue resource, covering 20 different tissue types and providing protein profiles for over 12,700 genes [2][3][4][5]. The 25.1 release notes do not restate the atlas-wide totals; the most recent stated coverage, from the 25.0 release, is over 27,800 antibodies targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes, about 88% of the human protein-coding genes [6].
