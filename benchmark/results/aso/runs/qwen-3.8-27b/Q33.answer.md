**Findings**

- Donor denominator: 5 donors (a, b, c, d, f) are represented in both NK-cell and memory CD8 T-cell sample data. All 384 CD-marker genes had complete coverage across all 5 matched donors, so all 384 genes were retained. (evidence: a17 row 0: distinct_Donor=5 | a20 row 0: gene=CD99, distinct_Donor_2=5, distinct_Donor=5)
- Spearman correlation between the paired median log2((NK+1)/(memCD8+1)) and the aggregate log2((NK+1)/(memCD8+1)) across all 384 retained genes is r = 0.8823 (p = 4.64 × 10⁻¹²⁷, n = 384). (evidence: a28 row 0: x=median_log2_ratio, y=agg_log2_ratio, method=spearman, n=384, r=0.8823, p_value=4.64e-127)
- Among the 290 genes with non-zero values in both the paired median and the aggregate ratio, 32 genes show opposite signs (paired median positive but aggregate negative, or vice versa). (evidence: a32 row 0: sum_opposite_sign=32)
- The 15 genes with the largest absolute disagreement between paired median and aggregate log2 ratio are listed in the table above. The largest disagreement is IL3RA (|Δ| = 2.58), where the paired median is 0.77 but the aggregate ratio is 3.35. (evidence: a30 row 0: gene=IL3RA, ensembl=ENSG00000185291, rank=1, disagreement=2.58219, median_log2_ratio=0.76831, min_log2_ratio=0.5161, max_log2_ratio=2.59451, mean_is_positive=1, agg_log2_ratio=3.3505, NK_agg=14.3, memCD8_agg=0.5)

**Limitations**

- The aggregate immune-cell file (rna_immune_cell.tsv) reports a single nTPM value per gene per cell type, likely a mean or median across all donors; its exact aggregation method is not stated in the file, so the paired-vs-aggregate comparison conflates donor-level variation with the aggregate's own averaging.
- Only 5 donors are represented in both cell types, limiting the statistical power of the paired analysis and the generalisability of per-donor statistics.
- The CD-marker gene set (384 genes) was selected by the HPA 'Protein class: CD markers' annotation, which may include some genes whose official symbol does not strictly follow the CD-number convention and may exclude some CD-named genes not annotated in that class.
- Exact-zero log2 ratios (nTPM = 0 in both cell types) were excluded from the opposite-sign count (290 of 384 genes retained for that calculation); these 94 genes contribute to the correlation but not to the sign-discordance count.
