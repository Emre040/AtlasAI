**Limitations**

- The hypergeometric test uses the full atlas gene set (20,162 genes) as the universe; it does not account for gene-length bias or other confounders that might affect tissue-specific expression annotation.
- Cohort 1 was selected with the 'Tissue enhanced' category (≥4-fold higher than the average of all other tissues) while Cohort 2 was selected with the 'Tissue enriched' category (≥4-fold higher in that tissue vs any other tissue). These are related but distinct thresholds in the atlas; the cohorts are therefore not defined by an identical criterion.
- The scatter plot (a11) and its underlying table (a9) include all 20,162 atlas genes; genes with blank nTPM in either tissue are plotted as missing and do not contribute to the mean calculations.
