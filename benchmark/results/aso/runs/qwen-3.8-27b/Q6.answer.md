**Limitations**

- 9 of the 123 partners have no main subcellular location annotation in the Human Protein Atlas and are excluded from the nuclear-fraction calculation.
- The prognostic table includes both TCGA discovery and independent validation cohorts; a gene may appear in both for the same cancer type.
- The 'validated' status reflects the Human Protein Atlas curation pipeline (discovery in one TCGA cohort confirmed in an independent validation cohort); it does not imply clinical validation.
