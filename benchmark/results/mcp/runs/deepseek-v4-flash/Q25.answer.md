**Enhanced validation methods applied for Western blot**
Per HPA's documentation (which follows the International Working Group for Antibody Validation, IWGAV, recommendations), Western blot antibodies are validated using the "enhanced validation" strategies, most relevantly:
- **Recombinant expression validation** – comparing the Western blot band against lysates from cells with the target knocked down/out or over-expressed (expected-size band checks), and
- **Independent antibodies** – confirming the band with a second antibody raised against a different region/epitope of the same protein.

(More broadly, HPA's enhanced validation toolbox comprises five methods – orthogonal validation, over-expression validation, independent antibodies, proximity ligation assay, and recombinant expression validation – but only the ones applicable to a given assay/antibody are used.)

**Orthogonal validation for immunohistochemistry**
For IHC, orthogonal validation compares the antibody staining pattern against an independent, antibody‑free readout – namely the **mRNA (transcriptomics) expression data generated by RNA‑seq** from the corresponding tissues. According to HPA's validation documentation, this comparison is carried out across the standard normal‑tissue panel, commonly cited as **44 normal tissues** ("antibody staining consistent with RNA expression data across 44 normal tissues").
