# Q23 (Easy; Documentation)

**Q:** Which human chromosome was the subject of the pilot study that preceded the Human Protein Atlas, in what year did that pilot begin, and what question did the founders ask in the 2002 funding proposal?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

While the Human Protein Atlas API tools provide access to protein- and gene-level data (such as expression, localization, pathology, and antibody validation) rather than historical project archives or grant proposals, the historical facts regarding the founding of the Human Protein Atlas are documented in HPA project retrospectives and publications:

1. **Pilot human chromosome:** **Chromosome 21** (chosen largely because it was one of the first human chromosomes to be fully sequenced, published in 2000).
2. **Year the pilot began:** **2001** (initiated by Mathias Uhlén and colleagues at the Royal Institute of Technology [KTH] and Uppsala University).
3. **Question asked in the 2002 funding proposal:** In the 2002 grant proposal submitted to the Knut and Alice Wallenberg (KAW) Foundation, the founders posed the central question:
   > **"Is it possible to generate antibodies to all human proteins?"** *(alternatively framed around whether it was feasible to systematically map and determine where all proteins are located throughout the human body)*. 

This proposal led to major funding approval and the formal launch of the Human Protein Atlas project in 2003.
