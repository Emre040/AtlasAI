# Q25 (Easy; Documentation)

**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply for Western blot, and for immunohistochemistry what does orthogonal validation compare the antibody staining against and across how many normal tissues?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the antibody validation principles established by the International Working Group for Antibody Validation (IWGAV) and implemented by the **Human Protein Atlas (HPA)**:

---

### 1. Enhanced Antibody Validation Methods for Western Blot
For Western blot (WB), the Human Protein Atlas applies two enhanced validation pillars:

1. **Genetic Validation**:
   - Compares protein detection in wild-type cells versus cells with target gene downregulation or inactivation (e.g., via **CRISPR-Cas9 knockout** or **siRNA knockdown**).
   - Specificity is validated when the band at the expected molecular weight is significantly reduced or completely absent in the modified cell line.

2. **Recombinant Expression**:
   - Evaluates the antibody against recombinant target proteins or cell lysates overexpressing the target protein (often with a tag, such as GFP or a peptide tag).
   - Specificity is confirmed when a band corresponding to the expected size of the overexpressed/recombinant target protein is detected.

---

### 2. Orthogonal Validation for Immunohistochemistry (IHC)
* **What it compares staining against**: 
  - Orthogonal validation compares the semi-quantitative antibody-mediated protein staining (IHC protein expression) against antibody-independent **RNA-seq data** (mRNA transcript expression levels).
* **Across how many normal tissues**: 
  - The comparison and correlation are evaluated across **44 normal human tissue types**.
