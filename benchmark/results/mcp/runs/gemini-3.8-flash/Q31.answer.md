### Why Consensus Is Not an Independent Third Replication Cohort

In the Human Protein Atlas:
1. **Mathematical Derivation from HPA and GTEx:** The consensus transcript expression level (consensus nTPM) for each gene and tissue is directly calculated from the individual datasets (internally normalized HPA and GTEx RNA-seq data, and previously FANTOM5). For each gene and tissue, the consensus value is defined as the maximum or normalized combination of the values from the individual sources.
2. **Shared Sample and Measurement Universe:** Consensus data does not originate from a separate, third biological cohort with newly collected donor tissues or independent sequencing libraries. It is entirely derived from the exact same raw data points, biological specimens, and sequencing experiments as the HPA and GTEx datasets.
3. **Correlation of Errors:** Any sample handling artifact, tissue composition bias, or library preparation error present in HPA or GTEx is propagated directly into the consensus dataset. Evaluating consensus against HPA or GTEx creates circularity rather than genuine external cross-cohort validation.
