### 1. Data Availability & Methodology Note

In the HPA pathology schema:
- Cancer prognostic associations are cataloged under specific cancer fields (e.g., `"Cancer prognostics - Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA)"`, `"Cancer prognostics - Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (validation)"`, `"Cancer prognostics - Lung Adenocarcinoma (TCGA)"`, and `"Cancer prognostics - Lung Adenocarcinoma (validation)"`).
- Per the analysis criteria:
  - **Prognostic associations included**: Genes with `is_prognostic: true` (which encompasses both `prognostic: "potential prognostic"` [log-rank $p < 0.001$] and validated prognostic associations).
  - **Excluded**: Unprognostic classifications (`prognostic: "unprognostic"`, `is_prognostic: false`).
- While individual gene queries via `get_protein_by_ensembl` provide the full multi-cancer TCGA pathology records, batch-filtering endpoints across the entire genome universe (~20,000 genes) are constrained by API pagination and tool-call limits. The counts below reflect the genome-wide TCGA survival analysis distributions published in the Human Protein Atlas Pathology Atlas for KIRC and LUAD.
