### 3. Estimating Survival Improvement from Observational Associations
* **Methodological & Data Limitation:** Calculating how much pharmacological or genetic inhibition of a gene would improve five-year patient survival **cannot be determined** from observational gene expression levels or prognostic association statistics:
  1. **Correlation vs. Causation:** Observational hazard ratios and Kaplan-Meier log-rank $p$-values reflect correlative biomarker relationships with disease state or progression, not the causal treatment effect size of molecular inhibition.

  3. **Absence of Interventional Data in HPA:** HPA does not host clinical interventional trial datasets or pharmacodynamic survival outcome modeling functions.
