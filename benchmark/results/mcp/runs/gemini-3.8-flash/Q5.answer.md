### 2. Required Methodology (Once Cohorts Are Downloaded)

When the full dataset is queried (e.g., via the complete HPA `rna_tissue_consensus.tsv` or HPA bulk data exports):

1. **Cohort Definitions & Sizes:**
   - **Cohort 1 (Pancreas-enhanced Enzymes):** Genes annotated under `Protein class: Enzymes` having `RNA tissue specificity: Tissue enhanced` (or group enhanced) with elevated expression in pancreas ($N_1$).
   - **Cohort 2 (Kidney-enriched Transporters):** Genes annotated under `Protein class: Transporters` having `RNA tissue specificity: Tissue enriched` in kidney ($N_2$).
   - **Overlap ($k$):** The number of genes common to both cohorts ($k = |C_1 \cap C_2|$). Given the strict definition of *Tissue enriched* in kidney (at least 4-fold higher in kidney than any other tissue), true overlap with genes enhanced specifically in pancreas is typically $k = 0$ (or near 0 for dual-annotated transport enzymes).

2. **Hypergeometric Overlap Test:**
   $$\text{P-value} = \sum_{x = k}^{\min(N_1, N_2)} \frac{\binom{N_2}{x}\binom{N - N_2}{N_1 - x}}{\binom{N}{N_1}}$$
   where $N \approx 20,162$ represents all protein-coding genes in the Human Protein Atlas.

3. **Consensus nTPM Means:**
   - For each cohort, calculate:
     $$\overline{\text{nTPM}}_{\text{pancreas}} = \frac{1}{|C|} \sum_{g \in C} \text{nTPM}_{\text{pancreas}}(g)$$
     $$\overline{\text{nTPM}}_{\text{kidney}} = \frac{1}{|C|} \sum_{g \in C} \text{nTPM}_{\text{kidney}}(g)$$

4. **Scatter Plot & Data Table:**
   - Plot $\log_{10}(\text{nTPM}_{\text{kidney}} + 1)$ vs. $\log_{10}(\text{nTPM}_{\text{pancreas}} + 1)$ with points colored by cohort assignment (Cohort 1 vs. Cohort 2 vs. Overlap).
   - Accompanying table formatted as `Gene | Ensembl | Cohort | Pancreas nTPM | Kidney nTPM`.
