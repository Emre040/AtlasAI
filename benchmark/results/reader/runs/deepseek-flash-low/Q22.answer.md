**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

The current release of the Human Protein Atlas is version 25.1 [1]. In the Single Cell resource, the latest addition is Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data: cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 different cell types, representing 14 different tissues, are integrated into the Single Cell resource [2]. The release also integrates quantitative proteomics profiles (MS-based) for bulk tissue samples into the Tissue resource, with 20 different tissue types overlapping the DVP samples integrated to the gene-summary pages [3]. For antibody and gene coverage, the release history gives the figures for the twenty-fifth major release (version 25.0): over 27,800 antibodies, targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes (~88% of the human protein-coding genes) [4].
