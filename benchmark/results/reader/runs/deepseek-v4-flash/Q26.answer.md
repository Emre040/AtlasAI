**Q:** Under which licence is the Human Protein Atlas released, and which publication (authors, journal, year and PubMed ID) does the atlas ask you to cite for its subcellular data?

The Human Protein Atlas is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, which covers all copyrightable parts of the database [1]. For subcellular data the atlas's list of primary publications gives the subcellular map paper as Thul PJ et al., A subcellular map of the human proteome, published in Science in 2017, PubMed 28495876 [2].

Sources:
[1] "The Human Protein Atlas is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License for all copyrightable parts of our database." — licence - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence)
[2] "Thul PJ et al., A subcellular map of the human proteome. Science. (2017) PubMed: 28495876 DOI: 10.1126/science.aal3321" — licence - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence)
