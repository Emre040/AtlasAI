**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

This release adds Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data to the Single Cell resource, providing cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 cell types (grouped into 24 classifications) and integrating quantitative proteomics profiles for 20 bulk tissue types, which together give protein profiles for over 12,700 genes [1]. The atlas now contains over 27,800 antibodies that target proteins from more than 17,400 human genes, covering about 88 % of the protein-coding genome [2].
