## Method

Counts come from the **cancer_prognostic_data** table (Human Protein Atlas bulk file), using the TCGA cohorts only. A gene counts as a "validated prognostic favourable/unfavourable" gene when the corresponding `validated prognostic – favourable` / `validated prognostic – unfavorable` field is non-empty.

"Tissue enriched in the tissue of origin" was determined by: (1) taking the HPA `RNA tissue specificity` label = **Tissue enriched** (from the proteinatlas table), and (2) requiring the gene's top-expressing tissue in the consensus normal-tissue dataset (`rna_tissue_consensus`) to be the cohort's tissue of origin (liver, kidney, pancreas, lung, breast, colon, stomach, prostate, ovary, thyroid gland, testis; for glioblastoma the brain-region tissues amygdala / basal ganglia / cerebellum / cerebral cortex / choroid plexus / hippocampal formation / hypothalamus / midbrain / spinal cord).
