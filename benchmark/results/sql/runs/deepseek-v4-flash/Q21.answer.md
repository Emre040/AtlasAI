## Method
"Tissue enriched" was computed from the consensus RNA bulk data (`rna_tissue_consensus`) using the HPA criterion: a gene is enriched in the tissue where its nTPM is the maximum and ≥ 4× the second-highest nTPM across tissues. "Secreted to blood" = `Secretome location = "Secreted to blood"` in the proteinatlas table. "FDA approved drug target" = `Protein class` containing `FDA approved drug targets`. In total, 3,130 enriched gene–tissue assignments were detected.
