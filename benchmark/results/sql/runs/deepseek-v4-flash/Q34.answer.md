## Method (brief)

- **Gene universe**: 5,573 Ensembl genes in the HPA master table (`proteinatlas`) whose `Protein class` contains "Predicted membrane proteins".
- **Normal IHC** (`normal_ihc_data`): Colon glandular cells, Breast glandular cells, Lung alveolar cells; Reliability = Enhanced or Supported only. Level → score: High=3, Medium=2, Low=1, Not detected=0; per gene/tissue the highest ordinal level was taken (each gene/tissue had ≤1 eligible record, so no aggregation was actually needed).
- **Cancer IHC** (`cancer_data`): colorectal cancer, breast cancer, lung cancer; rows with ≥5 observations (High+Medium+Low+Not detected). Weighted score = (3·High + 2·Medium + Low)/(High+Medium+Low+Not detected).
- **Change** = cancer weighted score − normal ordinal score, computed only for genes having both measurements.
- Medians are DuckDB `median()`; fractions are counts of change ≥ 1 divided by the number of genes with both measurements.
- **Important caveat**: normal IHC and cancer IHC are aggregate ordinal summaries over *different* tissue/cancer specimens; these are not paired-patient measurements, and all score differences are cross-sample ordinal summaries.
