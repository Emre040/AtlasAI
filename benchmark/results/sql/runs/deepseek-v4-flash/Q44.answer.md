## 4. Derived values

**Important caveat (stated, not estimated):** the provided HPA bulk files give RNA as nTPM (a per-million normalised abundance) and protein as label-free MS Intensity (arbitrary, proportional-to-molecule-count units) plus CPTAC log2 fold-changes. They contain **no per-cell absolute calibration** (no total mRNA or total protein molecules per cell, no cell counts, no standards). Therefore a true “absolute protein molecules per cell” (molecules/cell) **cannot be computed from these files alone**. What is fully determined is the abundance on the native scale, which is **proportional to molecules per cell** within normal lung, and the tumour value **normal × 2^logFC** (CPTAC tumour/normal protein fold change). The protein:RNA ratios below are cross-scale indices (MS-Intensity units per nTPM unit), proportional to the true protein-molecule-per-RNA-molecule ratio with a shared, unknown constant. Rankings and log-differences are unaffected by this constant.
