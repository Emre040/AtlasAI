## 1. Protein set and computation

Proteins with a blood concentration measured **by both immunoassay (IM) and mass spectrometry (MS)** = rows in `proteinatlas` where both `Blood concentration - Conc. blood IM [pg/L]` and `Blood concentration - Conc. blood MS [pg/L]` are numeric.

- **Number of proteins: 316**
- For each protein I computed `log10(IM conc)` and `log10(MS conc)` from the pg/L values, and the discordance metric `log10 ratio = log10(IM/MS) = log10(IM) − log10(MS)` (positive ⇒ IM higher; negative ⇒ MS higher).

## 2. Scatter (immunoassay vs mass spectrometry, colored by secretome location)

- x‑axis: `log10(MS conc [pg/L])`; y‑axis: `log10(IM conc [pg/L])`
- **Secretome location is identical for all 316 proteins: `Secreted to blood`.** Therefore the scatter would be a single color/group (no multi‑location separation is possible in these data).
- Pearson correlation between `log10(IM)` and `log10(MS)` over the 316 points: **r = 0.804**
- Mean `log10(IM)` = **8.062**, mean `log10(MS)` = **7.742**
- Direction counts: **219 proteins** have IM ≥ MS (log10 ratio ≥ 0); **97 proteins** have MS > IM.
