### 5. Overlap Test

By definition in the Human Protein Atlas, a "Lineage enriched" gene has at least a four-fold higher expression level in a single lineage than in any other lineage, making enrichment in NK-cells alone and B-cells alone mutually exclusive.

#### $2 \times 2$ Contingency Table (Universe = 2,197 lineage enriched genes)

| | B-cells Enriched | Not B-cells Enriched | Total |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **NK-cells Enriched** | **0** | **65** | **65** |
| **Not NK-cells Enriched** | **301** | **1,831** | **2,132** |
| **Total** | **301** | **1,896** | **2,197** |

#### Test Statistics
* **Observed Overlap:** **0 genes**
* **Expected Overlap:** $\frac{65 \times 301}{2,197} \approx \mathbf{8.91\text{ genes}}$
* **Odds Ratio:** **0**
* **Hypergeometric / Fisher's Exact Test $p$-value (Depletion / Mutual Exclusion):**
  $$p = \frac{\binom{301}{0}\binom{1,896}{65}}{\binom{2,197}{65}} \approx \mathbf{5.94 \times 10^{-5}}$$
* **Conclusion:** The two cohorts are significantly mutually exclusive ($p < 0.0001$), with zero overlapping genes.
