# Q22 (Easy; Documentation)

**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Human Protein Atlas database:

### 1. Release Version & New Data
* **Release Version:** **Human Protein Atlas version 24** (reflecting data updates into 2024/2025).
* **Major New Data Added:**
  * **Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP):** Single-cell resolution spatial mass spectrometry proteomics data across tissue cell types (`dvp_cell_type`, `dvp_cell_types`, `dvp_cell_type_group_data`, and protein cell type specificity classifications).
  * **CPTAC Cancer Proteomics:** Integration of clinical proteogenomics data from the Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium (`cancer_cptac`, `cancer_cptac_cancers`) covering 11 cancer types.
  * **Expanded Blood Disease Profiling (PEA):** Proximity Extension Assay profiling across **60 diseases** (`blood_pea_diseases`, `blood_pea_disease_de`).
  * **Subcellular Microscopy UMAP & Deep Features:** Cell profiling with image-derived deep feature representations across 446 features (`subcell_image_umap_features`).
  * **Consensus Protein-Protein Interactions:** Curated interaction networks integrating IntAct, BioGRID, and OpenCell (`interaction_consensus`).
  * **Expanded Single-Cell & Transcriptomic Datasets:** New single-nuclei and single-cell RNA-seq datasets (including updated brain, adipose, adrenal, and pancreas data) and transcript-level resolution expression.

### 2. Number of Genes and Antibodies Covered
* **Human Genes Covered:** **20,162** Ensembl gene entries (20,151 distinct gene names).
* **Antibodies Covered:**
  * **27,883** distinct antibodies (with **27,883** distinct RRIDs) mapped across genes.
  * These antibodies cover **17,407** human genes in total.
