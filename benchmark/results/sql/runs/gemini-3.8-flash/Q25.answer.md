# Q25 (Easy; Documentation)

**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply for Western blot, and for immunohistochemistry what does orthogonal validation compare the antibody staining against and across how many normal tissues?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

In the Human Protein Atlas (HPA), enhanced antibody validation is based on the pillars proposed by the International Working Group for Antibody Validation (IWGAV):

### 1. Enhanced Antibody Validation Methods for Western Blot
For Western blot (WB), the Human Protein Atlas applies two primary enhanced validation strategies:
* **Genetic validation:** Validation by genetic down-regulation (knockdown using siRNA) or gene knockout (using CRISPR-Cas9), demonstrating a corresponding reduction or loss of the target band on the Western blot.
* **Recombinant expression:** Validation by expressing the target protein—typically using over-expression lysates (with or without a tag, such as Myc-FLAG/DDK)—to confirm the presence of a band at the predicted molecular weight.

---

### 2. Orthogonal Validation for Immunohistochemistry (IHC)
* **What it compares against:** Orthogonal validation compares the antibody staining intensity/protein expression profile against **RNA-seq data** (transcript/mRNA expression levels of the corresponding gene).
* **Across how many normal tissues:** The comparison is performed across **44 normal human tissues**.
