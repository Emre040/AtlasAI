### 2. Overlap and Hypergeometric Test

- **Total genes in atlas background ($N$):** 20,162 genes
- **Cohort 1 size ($K$):** 36 genes
- **Cohort 2 size ($n$):** 29 genes
- **Observed overlap ($k$):** **0 genes**
- **Expected overlap by chance:** 
  $$\mathbb{E}[X] = \frac{K \times n}{N} = \frac{36 \times 29}{20{,}162} \approx 0.0518\text{ genes}$$
- **Hypergeometric test ($p$-value for over-representation, $P(X \ge 0)$):** **$1.0$** *(probability of 0 overlap under the null is $\approx 0.9495$)*.

**Conclusion:** The two cohorts **do not** overlap more than expected by chance. Because HPA tissue specificity categories are mutually exclusive per gene, no gene is simultaneously pancreas-enhanced and kidney-enriched.
