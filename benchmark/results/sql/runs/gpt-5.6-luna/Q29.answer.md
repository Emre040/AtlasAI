## Interpretation and causal caveat

These are **proteomic disease associations** from blood proximity-extension assay measurements relative to healthy controls. Concordant or opposing logFC values describe differences in circulating protein-associated signals between disease groups; they do **not** establish that a protein causes RA or SLE, nor that changing the protein would alter disease risk or progression. Causal interpretation would require additional evidence, such as longitudinal data, randomized intervention, or appropriately designed genetic causal-inference analyses.
