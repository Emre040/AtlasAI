## Absolute molecules per cell and protein:RNA ratios

These requested quantities **cannot be calculated from the supplied HPA tables**. Specifically:

1. **RNA nTPM is a normalized relative RNA-abundance measure**, not RNA molecules per cell.
2. **MS Intensity is an MS signal/intensity measure**, not protein molecules per cell.
3. The available CPTAC **logFC** is a relative tumour-versus-normal protein change, not an absolute tumour concentration.
4. The tool data contain **no sample-/gene-specific calibration factors** to convert nTPM to RNA molecules/cell or MS Intensity to protein molecules/cell.

Therefore, exact values for:

- normal-lung **protein molecules per cell**,
- lung-adenocarcinoma **protein molecules per cell**,
- normal or tumour **protein-to-RNA molecule ratios**,
- a ranking by **tumour molecules per cell**, and
- an **absolute-abundance comparison / molecule-ratio heatmap**

are not determinable without external calibration data. Producing them would require unsupported assumptions and would not be absolute.
