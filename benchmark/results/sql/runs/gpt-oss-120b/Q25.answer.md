**Enhanced antibody‑validation methods that the Human Protein Atlas (HPA) uses for **Western‑blot** antibodies**

| Validation method | What it does (in brief) |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Western‑blot** | The antibody is tested on a SDS‑PAGE/​Western‑blot of a cell‑line or tissue lysate; the band(s) are compared with the expected molecular weight of the target protein. |
| **Protein‑array (antibody‑array)** | The antibody is probed on a protein‑array that contains many recombinant human proteins; the binding pattern is inspected for specificity. |
| **Mass‑spectrometry (MS)‑based protein profiling** | The protein band detected by the antibody on a Western‑blot is excised and identified by MS; the peptide spectrum is matched to the target protein to confirm that the band truly represents the intended protein. |
| **Knock‑down / knock‑out (RNAi or CRISPR) validation** | Cells in which the target gene has been silenced or knocked‑out are analysed by Western‑blot; loss of the band confirms antibody specificity. |

These four approaches are listed by the HPA as the **“enhanced” validation methods** for Western‑blot antibodies.

**Orthogonal validation for immunohistochemistry (IHC)**

The comparison is performed **across the complete set of normal human tissues** that are profiled by the HPA. The dataset contains **44 distinct normal tissues** (e.g., brain, heart, liver, lung, kidney, etc.), so the orthogonal IHC validation is carried out **across 44 normal tissues**.
