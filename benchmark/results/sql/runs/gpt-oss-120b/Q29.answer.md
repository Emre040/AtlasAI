**5.  Interpretation notes**

* **Proteomic association vs. causation** – The presence of a gene in the blood immuno‑assay or MS dataset indicates that the protein has been **detected** in blood (either by antibody‑based assay or mass‑spectrometry).  This is an *association* (the protein is measurable in the sample) and does **not** prove that the protein **causes** the disease phenotype.  Functional validation would be required to move from association to causation.

* **Shared regulation** – The large majority of significant genes are concordantly regulated (202 up, 13 down), reflecting a strong common inflammatory signature between RA and SLE.

* **Opposing genes** – Only four genes show opposite regulation (down in RA, up in SLE or vice‑versa).  These may highlight disease‑specific pathways and merit further experimental follow‑up.

* **Correlation** – A Spearman ρ = 0.76 indicates a strong monotonic relationship between the two disease logFC profiles, consistent with the high number of shared regulated genes.
