For each, compute the ratio of hypothalamus nTPM to cerebral cortex nTPM and draw a bar chart of the fifteen highest, a heatmap of those fifteen across all brain regions on a log scale, the tissue outside the brain with the highest consensus nTPM for each gene, and a bar chart of the cohort's main subcellular locations. ## Method / cohort definition
- **Cohort** = genes labelled **RNA brain regional specificity = "Region enriched"** in the Human Protein Atlas (atlas summary table) whose region is the **hypothalamus** → **38 genes**.
- **Hypothalamus & cerebral cortex nTPM** come from the `rna_brain_region_hpa` table (HPA brain regional RNA).
- **Ratio** = hypothalamus nTPM ÷ cerebral cortex nTPM.
- **Non-brain top tissue** = the tissue with the highest nTPM in `rna_tissue_consensus` restricted to the 37 non-brain organs.
- **Main subcellular location** from the atlas summary (`Subcellular main location`).
