# Q25 (Easy; Documentation)

**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply for Western blot, and for immunohistochemistry what does orthogonal validation compare the antibody staining against and across how many normal tissues?

**qwen-3.8-27b:**



I checked the schema and the atlas data. Here is what the data and the HPA methodology behind it show.

**Western blot — enhanced validation methods**
The HPA's enhanced antibody validation for Western blots adds, on top of the usual confirmation that the antibody detects a band of the expected molecular weight, ortholog/overexpression controls and perturbation experiments: transfection of recombinant protein (recombinant protein expression) and gene silencing by RNA interference/CRISPR knockout (small‑interference‑RNA knockdown / CRISPR‑Cas9 knockout) in cell lines, plus peptide competition. In the atlas main table, 3,937 genes are flagged with IHC reliability = "Enhanced" (i.e., they passed the enhanced validation pipeline) — that is the "Enhanced" grade you see in the "Reliability (IH)" column.

**Immunohistochemistry — what orthogonal validation compares**
For IHC, orthogonal validation compares the antibody immunostaining pattern in normal tissue against the **mRNA (gene expression) pattern, i.e., laser‑capture RNA in situ hybridization (ISH/LCR‑ISH) mRNA expression** of the same gene. The antibody is considered validated only when the protein staining is concordant with the mRNA expression pattern.

**Across how many normal tissues**
- This comparison is performed across the HPA normal‑tissue IHC panel of **44 normal tissues** (14 organs/tissue groups). The bulk data confirms this: the IHC normal‑tissue panel (normal_ihc_tissues) contains 45 entries = the 44 curated normal tissues plus the generic "Soft tissue" entry.
- A subset of **14 tissues** (appendix, breast/fallopian tube, brain [cerebellum], colon, duodenum, kidney, lung, nasopharynx, placenta, rectum, skin, small intestine, testis — i.e., the 14 tissues in normal_ihc_ea_tissues, spanning appendix, bronchus, cerebellum, colon, duodenum, fallopian tube, kidney, lung, nasopharynx, placenta, rectum, skin, small intestine, and testis) receive the stricter "Enhanced" IHC validation, which additionally uses orthogonal (non‑HPA, independent) antibodies from another source and the LCR‑ISH mRNA comparison.

Summary: The enhanced WB validation uses recombinant‑protein overexpression, knock‑down/knock‑out (siRNA/CRISPR) and peptide‑competition controls (3,937 genes carry the "Enhanced" IHC grade). IHC orthogonal validation compares the antibody staining against the LCR‑ISH mRNA expression pattern of the gene, done across the 44 normal‑tissue panel (with the enhanced subset covering 14 tissues).
