## 6. Interpretation caveat

These are **ordinal summaries computed on independent sample sets** — normal-tissue IHC (curated cell-type scores) versus cancer-tissue IHC (sensitivity/counts across a set of tumor samples) — from different cohorts of patients. The scatter "pairs" are per-gene score pairs across two different populations, **not measurements from the same patients**. Consequently, the cancer-minus-normal differences (median 0 in all three pairs; +1 unit or more in 3.7–7.3 % of genes) describe population-level ordinal expression shifts at best, and individual-gene changes such as CLDN1 +2.417 should not be read as paired patient-level fold-changes.
