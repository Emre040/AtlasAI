## License

The Human Protein Atlas is released under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)**. The site states it is "licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License for all copyrightable parts of our database," while noting that some data may be subject to third-party constraints, for which users are responsible.
Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence

## Publication to cite for the subcellular data

For the subcellular (subcellular map of the human proteome) data, the page asks you to cite the following **primary publication**:

- **Authors:** Thul PJ et al. (Peter J. Thul and colleagues)
- **Title:** "A subcellular map of the human proteome"
- **Journal:** *Science*
- **Year:** 2017
- **PubMed ID:** 28495876 (DOI: 10.1126/science.aal3321)

Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence (Primary publications section, second entry)

For completeness, the page's citation rules state you must include a reference to a primary publication (or another listed publication) **and** reference the website "Human Protein Atlas proteinatlas.org"; for specific images/genes/data you should also provide the direct URL pointing to that content (© Human Protein Atlas, available from v##.proteinatlas.org).
