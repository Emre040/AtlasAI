## Western blot — enhanced antibody validation methods

According to the Human Protein Atlas antibody validation page, the enhanced antibody validation strategies used for **Western blot** are five:

1. **Genetic validation** (siRNA knock-down/knock-out)
2. **Recombinant expression validation**
3. **Independent antibody validation**
4. **Orthogonal validation** (comparing band intensity against protein levels quantified by mass spectrometry, or against corresponding RNA)
5. **Capture MS validation** (comparing the molecular weight of the stained band with the protein size obtained by a capture MS method)

The page states: "Western blot: Genetic validation, Recombinant expression validation, Independent antibody validation, Orthogonal validation, Capture MS validation," with detailed descriptions under the "Enhanced antibody validation - WB" section.

Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation

## Immunohistochemistry — orthogonal validation

For immunohistochemistry, the enhanced validation methods are **Orthogonal validation** and **Independent antibody validation**. Regarding orthogonal validation specifically, the page states:

> "Orthogonal validation is based on manual evaluation of the correlation between the staining intensity of a single-target antibody and corresponding **mRNA levels** across **up to 46 normal tissues**. For single-target antibodies where consistency between staining intensity and mRNA levels is scored as high or medium, two representative images corresponding to tissues with high and low expression of protein and mRNA are selected. If the difference in mRNA levels between the two representative tissues is at least 4-fold, the antibody will receive the validation score 'Enhanced'."

So, immunohistochemistry orthogonal validation compares the antibody staining (intensity) against the **corresponding mRNA levels**, across **up to 46 normal tissues** (the difference between the two representative tissues must be at least 4-fold).

Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation

(Note: the related standard IHC validation section describes literature conformity and RNA consistency/paired-antibody similarity assessed in *44* normal tissues, distinct from the "up to 46 normal tissues" figure used in the enhanced orthogonal validation.)
