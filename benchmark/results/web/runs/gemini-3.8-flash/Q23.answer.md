**Q:** Which human chromosome was the subject of the pilot study that preceded the Human Protein Atlas, in what year did that pilot begin, and what question did the founders ask in the 2002 funding proposal?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Human Protein Atlas history pages:

* **Chromosome and start year of the pilot study:** 
  The pilot study that preceded the Human Protein Atlas focused on human **chromosome 21** and was initiated in the year **2000**.  
  *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/history/2000+Chromosome+21+pilot)*

* **Question asked in the 2002 funding proposal:** 
  In the 2002 funding proposal submitted to the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation, the founders asked:  
  > *"What is it that makes a kidney a kidney? And what makes a heart a heart?"*  
  
  *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/history/2003+Start+of+the+Human+Protein+Atlas+program)*
