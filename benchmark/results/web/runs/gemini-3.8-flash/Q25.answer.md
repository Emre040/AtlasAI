# Q25 (documentation)

**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply for Western blot, and for immunohistochemistry what does orthogonal validation compare the antibody staining against and across how many normal tissues?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

According to the Human Protein Atlas (**Methods: Antibody validation**, https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation):

### 1. Enhanced antibody validation methods for Western blot
The Human Protein Atlas applies five enhanced validation strategies for Western blot:
* **Genetic validation** (e.g., using siRNA or CRISPR knock-down/knock-out)
* **Recombinant expression validation** (over-expression of the target protein)
* **Independent antibody validation** (comparison with an independent antibody targeting a non-overlapping epitope)
* **Orthogonal validation** (comparison against protein levels quantified by mass spectrometry or against corresponding RNA levels)
* **Capture MS validation** (comparison of the stained band size with proteomics/capture mass spectrometry)

---

### 2. Orthogonal validation for immunohistochemistry
* **What it compares the antibody staining against:** It compares the staining intensity of a single-target antibody against **corresponding mRNA levels**.
* **Across how many normal tissues:** It evaluates this correlation across **up to 46 normal tissues**.
