# Q25 (documentation)

**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply for Western blot, and for immunohistochemistry what does orthogonal validation compare the antibody staining against and across how many normal tissues?

I found all the details on the HPA "Antibody validation" methods page (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation).

## Western blot — enhanced antibody validation methods

According to the page, enhanced validation follows the five "pillars" of the International Working Group for Antibody Validation (Uhlen M et al. 2016), and for Western blot the HPA applies five specific strategies:

1. **Genetic validation** (siRNA/CRISPR knock-down — signal downregulation ≥25%)
2. **Recombinant expression validation** (over-expression of the target in a cell line)
3. **Independent antibody validation** (comparing two antibodies with non-overlapping epitopes)
4. **Orthogonal validation** (comparing band intensity against protein levels quantified by mass spectrometry, or against RNA)
5. **Capture MS validation** (comparing the antibody's band size to the protein size determined by capture mass spectrometry)

(https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)

## Immunohistochemistry — orthogonal validation

For IHC, **orthogonal validation compares the intensity of antibody staining with the corresponding mRNA (transcript) levels** — i.e., against gene expression data rather than another antibody. It is performed across **up to 46 normal tissues**. If the staining–mRNA consistency is scored as high or medium, two representative tissues (high vs. low expression) are selected, and the antibody receives the "Enhanced" score if the mRNA levels in those two tissues differ by at least **4-fold**.

(https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)

**Final answer:** For Western blot, the HPA's enhanced validation comprises genetic (siRNA/knock-down), recombinant expression, independent antibody, orthogonal (mass spectrometry/RNA-based), and capture MS validation. For IHC, orthogonal validation compares antibody staining intensity against mRNA expression levels across up to 46 normal tissues (with an "Enhanced" score requiring high/medium consistency and ≥4-fold mRNA difference between representative high- and low-expression tissues).
