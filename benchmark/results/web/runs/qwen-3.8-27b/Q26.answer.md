## Final answer

**Licence:** The Human Protein Atlas is released under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)** "for all copyrightable parts of our database," with a note that some data subject to third-party constraints may be included and that users are responsible for complying with third-party restrictions (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence).

**Publication to cite for subcellular data:** The atlas's "Primary publications" citation list (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence) lists, as the subcellular resource's primary publication:

- **Authors (as listed by the atlas):** Thul PJ et al. (i.e., Thul as first author, with many co-authors — the full author list is not spelled out on the page I could open)
- **Title:** "A subcellular map of the human proteome"
- **Journal:** *Science*
- **Year:** 2017
- **PubMed ID:** **28495876** (DOI: 10.1126/science.aal3321)

The licence page states that if you cite or display any content of the atlas, you must include a reference to one of its primary publications (the subcellular map paper for subcellular data) **and** a reference to the website (Human Protein Atlas, proteinatlas.org). If you use specific images, genes, or downloaded data, you should additionally cite the specific item with its direct URL and image credit "Human Protein Atlas."

Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence (subcellular paper entry and licence statement).
