**Limitations**

- Tissue enrichment is an operational classification based on RNA consensus expression (at least fourfold higher mRNA level in a given tissue compared to any other tissue), meaning highly specific genes are mutually exclusive by definition.
- Prognostic evaluations are based on TCGA bulk RNA-sequencing cohorts and survival associations, which reflect statistical correlation with patient overall survival rather than direct causal or therapeutic effects.
- Secretome annotations represent computational prediction and experimental curation in the HPA database and are limited to blood secretion annotations.
