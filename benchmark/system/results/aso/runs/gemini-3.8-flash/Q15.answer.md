**Limitations**

- The atlas records blood concentrations estimated from immunoassay and mass spectrometry platforms that differ in sensitivity, dynamic range, and antibody or peptide targeting specificity; discrepancies between platforms do not prove biological isoform differences or technical artifacts.
- Tissue enrichment is classified based on bulk RNA expression data (nTPM) and may not directly reflect protein translation or tissue-specific secretion rates.
