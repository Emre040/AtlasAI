**Limitations**

- Eighteen of the 38 hypothalamus enriched genes lack subcellular location annotations in the HPA subcellular location dataset, so subcellular distribution percentages reflect only the subset with experimental data.
- Tissue and brain region expression levels represent consensus RNA nTPM values, which reflect transcript abundance rather than translated protein quantity or functional secretion.
- Where ties occurred at zero nTPM in tissues outside the brain (e.g., GSX1 and FOXB2 having 0.0 nTPM across multiple non-brain tissues), the top outside tissue was selected deterministically.
