**Limitations**

- Consensus RNA expression (nTPM) and mass-spectrometry tissue intensity are relative quantification measurements from distinct experimental platforms; absolute molecular abundances, copy numbers, protein-to-RNA stoichiometric ratios, and causal post-transcriptional mechanisms cannot be established from these data.
- Zero intensity or non-recorded protein levels in tissue mass-spectrometry do not prove complete absence of protein expression, representing assay detection limits or unmeasured proteins.
