**Limitations**

- Consensus tissue RNA nTPM is a composite summary of HPA and GTEx expression measurements, not an independently generated biological cohort; hence agreement between individual datasets and consensus cannot serve as validation.
- Differences between HPA and GTEx measurements reflect biological donor variability, tissue sampling protocols (e.g. surgical resections vs post-mortem donors), and RNA extraction/sequencing batch methodologies rather than functional transcript differences.
- Transcriptional abundance (nTPM) does not establish protein abundance, DNA binding activity, or regulatory efficacy of transcription factors.
