**Limitations**

- Immunohistochemistry scores are ordinal summaries derived from semi-quantitative antibody staining across distinct patient cohorts, not paired-patient measurements or absolute molecular concentrations.
- A missing record in normal IHC or cancer IHC reflects an unmeasured condition or filtered reliability tier, not proof of gene absence or zero protein expression.
- Cancer IHC weighted scores reflect cohort proportions across ordinal categories (High, Medium, Low, Not detected) and cannot be interpreted as absolute copy numbers, stoichiometry, or direct causal mechanisms in oncogenesis.
