**Limitations**

- The Human Protein Atlas prognostic tables record categorical directional associations and log-rank/prognostic designations rather than raw clinical survival curves, so prognostic effect sizes cannot be directly compared across different cancer cohorts.
- Validation cohorts are absent or partially missing for certain genes in Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma (704 genes with missing validation records in HPA), which were treated explicitly as distinct missing cohort entries rather than absent associations. (704: not verified against the data of this study)
- CPTAC proteomic coverage is limited to a subset of genes (~11,725 genes), resulting in unmeasured protein logFC and adjusted p-values for several candidates (RAD51, FOXM1, HROB, C1orf112). (11,725: not verified against the data of this study)
