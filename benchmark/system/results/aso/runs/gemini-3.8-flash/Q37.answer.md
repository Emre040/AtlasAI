**Limitations**

- The Human Protein Atlas interaction_consensus dataset contains consensus interaction pairs curated from high-throughput interaction databases; absent edges indicate lack of recorded consensus interactions rather than physical impossibility of binding.
- RNA consensus expression (nTPM) measures steady-state transcript abundance and does not directly measure absolute protein concentrations or molecule counts.
- Subcellular localization annotations depend on antibody-based confocal immunofluorescence profiling; unannotated entries represent absence of data rather than absence of protein from cellular compartments.
