**Limitations**

- Prognostic associations from TCGA bulk RNA sequencing reflect statistical survival correlations rather than demonstrated causal mechanisms or therapeutic efficacy.
- RNA consensus nTPM levels reflect baseline physiological tissue abundance rather than tumour-specific expression profiles or absolute molecular copy counts.
