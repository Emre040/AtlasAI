**Limitations**

- Prognostic associations from TCGA RNA-seq cohorts reflect statistical survival correlations rather than causal biological relationships or therapeutic responses.
- The analysis includes both validated and potential prognostic categories, which represent different statistical tiers of prognostic significance in the Human Protein Atlas.
- Gene universe is defined by the 19,973 Ensembl gene entries evaluated in the HPA pathology prognostic atlas.
