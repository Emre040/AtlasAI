**Limitations**

- Observational differential protein expression levels (logFC, adjusted p-value) and cohort prognostic p-values reflect statistical associations across clinical cohorts, not interventional effect sizes or causal treatment mechanisms.
- Neither 5-year patient survival improvements nor optimal clinical treatment targets can be quantitatively derived from observational hazard/expression statistics without prospective or functional perturbation trials.
