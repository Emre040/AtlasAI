**Findings**

- The liver-enriched cohort contains 267 genes, the kidney-enriched cohort 53, and the pancreas-enriched cohort 62. (evidence: a38 row 0: cohort=liver, count=267; row 1: cohort=kidney, count=53; row 2: cohort=pancreas, count=62)
- The share of genes secreted to blood is highest in the liver cohort (0.475655), followed by pancreas (0.0967742) and kidney (0.0754717). (evidence: a38 row 0: cohort=liver, mean_is_blood=0.475655; row 1: cohort=kidney, mean_is_blood=0.0754717; row 2: cohort=pancreas, mean_is_blood=0.0967742)
- The share of genes with a validated favourable prognosis in the matching TCGA cancer is highest in the kidney cohort (0.301887), intermediate in liver (0.168539), and zero in pancreas (0). (evidence: a38 row 0: cohort=liver, mean_is_favorable=0.168539; row 1: cohort=kidney, mean_is_favorable=0.301887; row 2: cohort=pancreas, mean_is_favorable=0)
- The median consensus nTPM in the cohort's own tissue is highest in pancreas (2320.8), intermediate in liver (408.5), and lowest in kidney (105.7). (evidence: a38 row 0: cohort=liver, median_nTPM=408.5; row 1: cohort=kidney, median_nTPM=105.7; row 2: cohort=pancreas, median_nTPM=2320.8)
- No gene is shared between any pair of the three cohorts: liver-kidney, liver-pancreas, and kidney-pancreas all have 0 shared genes against a universe of 20162 genes (hypergeometric p = 1 for each pair). (evidence: a42 row 0: pair=Liver vs Kidney overlap, shared=0, universe=20162, p_value=1; row 1: pair=Liver vs Pancreas overlap, shared=0, universe=20162, p_value=1; row 2: pair=Kidney vs Pancreas overlap, shared=0, universe=20162, p_value=1)

**Limitations**

- The 'tissue enriched' category in the Human Protein Atlas is defined by RNA expression (≥4-fold higher in the tissue of interest vs any other tissue); it does not guarantee protein-level enrichment.
- The favourable-prognosis share uses only the 'validated prognostic favorable' category from the TCGA prognostics column; genes annotated as 'potential prognostic favorable' are not counted.
- The heatmap displays the 15 highest-expressed genes per cohort in their own tissue; these may not be the 15 highest in the other two tissues, so cross-tissue comparisons within the heatmap are limited to this selected subset.
- The grouped bar chart plots mean_is_blood, mean_is_favorable, and median_nTPM on the same axis; because median_nTPM is on a very different scale (hundreds to thousands) than the two shares (0–1), the bar for median_nTPM dominates visually.
