**Findings**

- 92 transcription factors have cell type enriched RNA expression in the Human Protein Atlas. Neutrophils is the cell type with the most such TFs (8), followed by Late spermatids and Oocytes (6 each). (evidence: a10 row 0: count=8, RNA single cell type specific nCPM_key=Neutrophils; row 1: count=6, RNA single cell type specific nCPM_key=Late spermatids; row 2: count=6, RNA single cell type specific nCPM_key=Oocytes)
- Among the 8 Neutrophil-enriched TFs, 5 have Low tissue specificity, 2 are Tissue enhanced, and 1 is Tissue enriched. (evidence: a16 row 0: count=5, RNA tissue specificity=Low tissue specificity; row 1: count=2, RNA tissue specificity=Tissue enhanced; row 2: count=1, RNA tissue specificity=Tissue enriched)
- Three of the 8 Neutrophil-enriched TFs (ELF2, STAT5B, ZNF429) have a validated favorable prognostic association in Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), with p-values of 6.792e-6, 1.266e-8, and 2.799e-4 respectively. None of the 8 has a validated unfavorable prognostic association in any TCGA cohort. (evidence: a15 row 0: gene=ELF2, direction=filter(artifact=a3, where=[{"column":"validated prognostic - favorable","op":"is_present"}]), Cancer=Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), validated prognostic - favorable=6.792e-6; row 4: gene=STAT5B, direction=filter(artifact=a3, where=[{"column":"validated prognostic - favorable","op":"is_present"}]), Cancer=Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), validated prognostic - favorable=1.266e-8; row 7: gene=ZNF429, direction=filter(artifact=a3, where=[{"column":"validated prognostic - favorable","op":"is_present"}]), Cancer=Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), validated prognostic - favorable=2.799e-4)

**Limitations**

- The 'RNA single cell type specific nCPM' column in proteinatlas.tsv records only the single top-enriched cell type per gene; a TF may be enriched in additional cell types at lower fold-change that are not captured here.
- The validated prognostic association columns record only the p-value threshold met (favorable or unfavorable); the specific statistical test, hazard ratio, and cohort size are not available in this release.
- The 92 TFs are those annotated under the protein class 'Transcription factors' in the Human Protein Atlas; TFs annotated under other protein classes (e.g. 'Transcription factor, nuclear') are excluded.
