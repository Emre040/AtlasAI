**Findings**

- Of the 10 liver-enriched genes with the highest liver consensus nTPM, only 4 have recorded interaction partners in the atlas: APOA1 (6 partners), APOA2 (2), HP (1), and ORM1 (1), for a total of 10 partner pairs. (evidence: a12 row 0: count=1, point=HP; row 1: count=1, point=ORM1; row 2: count=2, point=APOA2; row 3: count=6, point=APOA1)
- The 10 partners span 7 main subcellular locations: Vesicles (2), Cytosol (2), Principal piece (1), Golgi apparatus (1), Plasma membrane (1), Nucleoplasm (1), and 2 partners with no recorded main location. (evidence: a11 row 0: count=2, Main location=Vesicles; row 1: count=2, Main location=—; row 2: count=2, Main location=Cytosol; row 3: count=1, Main location=Principal piece; row 4: count=1, Main location=Golgi apparatus; row 5: count=1, Main location=Plasma membrane; row 6: count=1, Main location=Nucleoplasm)
- Partner liver nTPM ranges from 0.0 (CATSPER1) to 55848.1 (HP); seed gene liver nTPM ranges from 20151.0 (APOA1) to 55848.1 (HP). (evidence: a20 row 0: nTPM=20151.0, nTPM_2=55848.1; row 3: nTPM=0.0, nTPM_2=33506.8; row 9: nTPM=55848.1, nTPM_2=20151.0)
- Five of the 10 partners are annotated as secreted to blood: APOA1, SERPINE1, APP, LCAT, and HP. (evidence: a20 row 0: gene=APOA1, Secretome location=Secreted to blood; row 1: gene=SERPINE1, Secretome location=Secreted to blood; row 5: gene=APP, Secretome location=Secreted to blood; row 8: gene=LCAT, Secretome location=Secreted to blood; row 9: gene=HP, Secretome location=Secreted to blood)

**Limitations**

- Only 4 of the 10 seed genes have consensus interaction partners recorded in this atlas release; the remaining 6 (including ALB, SAA1, and others) have no rows in interaction_consensus and are therefore absent from the partner table.
- Two partners (SERPINE1 and SNCA) have no recorded main subcellular location in the atlas.
- The scatter plot includes one point at partner nTPM = 0.0 (CATSPER1), which is not plottable on a log axis and may appear at the axis limit or be omitted depending on rendering.
