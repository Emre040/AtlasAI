**Findings**

- Testis has the most tissue-enriched genes (932), followed by brain (475) and liver (267). (evidence: a10 row 26: count=932 | a10 row 3: count=475 | a10 row 13: count=267)
- Salivary gland has the highest share of secreted proteins (81.4%), followed by epididymis (69.8%). (evidence: a10 row 23: mean_Secreted=0.813953 | a10 row 6: mean_Secreted=0.697917)
- Prostate has the highest share of FDA approved drug targets (21.4%), followed by kidney (18.9%). (evidence: a10 row 21: mean_FDA=0.214286 | a10 row 12: mean_FDA=0.188679)
- Testis is overwhelmingly dominated by Predicted intracellular proteins (787 of 932 enriched genes, 84.3%), consistent with its role in spermatogenesis and intracellular gene expression. (evidence: a21 | a10 row 26: count=932)
- Liver shows the most balanced distribution across protein classes, with Predicted intracellular proteins (153) and Plasma proteins (152) the most represented, reflecting its dual metabolic and secretory roles. (evidence: a21)
- The 8 most common protein classes among enriched genes in the top 10 tissues are: Predicted intracellular proteins, Predicted membrane proteins, Plasma proteins, Enzymes, Metabolic proteins, Disease related genes, Human disease related genes, and Predicted secreted proteins. (evidence: a19 row 0: Protein class item_item=Predicted intracellular proteins; row 1: Protein class item_item=Predicted membrane proteins; row 2: Protein class item_item=Disease related genes; row 3: Protein class item_item=Predicted secreted proteins; row 4: Protein class item_item=Human disease related genes; row 5: Protein class item_item=Plasma proteins; row 6: Protein class item_item=Enzymes; row 7: Protein class item_item=Metabolic proteins)

**Limitations**

- Tissue enrichment is defined as ≥4-fold higher mRNA in one tissue versus any other; the count reflects genes meeting this threshold in the consensus RNA dataset and does not capture lower-level tissue specificity.
- The 'secreted' flag is based on the 'Predicted secreted proteins' annotation in the Protein class column, which reflects computational prediction rather than experimental confirmation of secretion into blood.
- The 'FDA approved drug target' flag reflects current FDA-approved targets as annotated in the atlas; newly approved or in-development targets are not included.
- The heatmap shows raw gene counts per tissue per protein class; a gene annotated with multiple protein classes is counted in each, so column sums exceed the tissue total.
