**Limitations**

- Three genes with exact-zero log2 ratios in both RNA and protein are excluded from the four direction combinations; they represent genes whose kidney and colon values are numerically identical for both measurements.
- CA1 (ENSG00000133742) and JAK1 (ENSG00000162434) have no secretome or subcellular main-location annotation in the current HPA release; these fields are blank in the detail table.
- The protein Intensity values are tissue-level summaries from mass spectrometry (ms_tissue); they reflect relative abundance and are not absolute concentrations.
- RNA nTPM values are consensus tissue-level estimates; individual sample-level variation is not captured.
