**Limitations**

- The CPTAC protein differential-expression data record logFC and adjusted p-values as relative measures; they do not provide absolute protein concentrations, copy numbers or molecule counts.
- RNA cancer specificity and FDA drug-target status are categorical annotations from the Human Protein Atlas master table; they are not derived from the CPTAC expression data and do not establish causal or therapeutic relationships.
- The Jaccard similarity is computed on the upregulated sets only (logFC ≥ 0.5, adjusted p < 0.01); it does not capture overlap in downregulated or total DE sets.
- The opposing-sign criterion requires a gene to be DE in ≥ 2 cancers with at least one up and one down call; genes DE in all three cancers with mixed directions are included, but the specific pattern (e.g., up-up-down vs up-down-up) is not further stratified in the reported count.
