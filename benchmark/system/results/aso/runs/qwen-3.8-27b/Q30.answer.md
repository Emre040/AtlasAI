**Findings**

- RNA–protein rank agreement is weak across all three cell types. Spearman r = 0.097 (cardiomyocytes, n=4714, p=2.25×10⁻¹¹), 0.354 (hepatocytes, n=5985, p=3.23×10⁻¹⁷⁶), and 0.153 (neutrophils, n=5396, p=1.79×10⁻²⁹). All correlations are statistically significant but indicate poor rank concordance between single-cell-type RNA nCPM and DVP cell-type Intensity. (evidence: a83 row 0: cell_type=cardiomyocytes, count=4714, r=0.0972, p_value=2.25e-11; row 1: cell_type=hepatocytes, count=5985, r=0.354, p_value=3.23e-176; row 2: cell_type=neutrophils, count=5396, r=0.1526, p_value=1.79e-29)
- Top-20 overlap is minimal: 3 of 20 genes shared (Jaccard = 0.081) for cardiomyocytes, 1 of 20 (Jaccard = 0.026) for hepatocytes, and 0 of 20 (Jaccard = 0) for neutrophils. The cardiomyocyte overlap genes are TTN, MYH7, and MYBPC3; the hepatocyte overlap gene is ALB. (evidence: a83 row 0: cell_type=cardiomyocytes, overlap=3, jaccard=0.0810811; row 1: cell_type=hepatocytes, overlap=1, jaccard=0.025641; row 2: cell_type=neutrophils, overlap=0, jaccard=0)

- Hepatocytes show the strongest (though still modest) RNA–protein agreement (r = 0.354), consistent with the liver's high abundance of secreted proteins (ALB, FGA, FGB, TTR, AMBP) that are well captured by both modalities. Neutrophils show the weakest top-20 overlap (zero shared genes), with the RNA top-20 dominated by mitochondrial genes (MT-CO3, MT-CO2, MT-CYB, MT-ATP6, MT-ND2, MT-ND1) and the protein top-20 dominated by histone variants (H4C5, H4C4, H4C1, H4C2) and structural proteins (MYH9, MYH14). (evidence: a83 row 1: cell_type=hepatocytes, r=0.354, overlap=1; row 2: cell_type=neutrophils, r=0.1526, overlap=0)

**Limitations**

- Spearman correlation measures rank concordance across all retained genes; it does not capture whether absolute expression levels agree or whether the relationship is linear on any scale.
- DVP cell-type Intensity is a deconvolved estimate from tissue-level imaging, not a direct single-cell measurement; its resolution and dynamic range differ fundamentally from scRNA-seq nCPM.
- The top-20 overlap and Jaccard metrics are sensitive to the arbitrary cutoff of 20; a different list size would yield different overlap values.
- Histone variant genes (H4C1–H4C15) appear as separate Ensembl entries with near-identical RNA nCPM values (all tied at the same rank), which inflates their representation in the discordant-gene list; they may represent a single biological signal split across paralog entries.
- Subcellular location annotations are available for only 6 of the 14 unique discordant genes; the remaining 8 have no annotation in the HPA subcellular location table.
- The analysis uses the HPA master table as the gene universe; genes absent from this table (e.g., some pseudogenes or recently annotated loci) are excluded.
