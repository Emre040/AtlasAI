**Limitations**

- The normal IHC level is a single ordinal call (highest level among Enhanced/Supported records) and does not capture the distribution of staining intensity across normal samples.
- The cancer IHC weighted score aggregates counts across all tumour samples for a given cancer type; it is not a per-patient measurement and cannot be directly compared to the normal level on a patient-by-patient basis.
- Genes with fewer than five cancer observations are excluded from the cancer score, which may bias the comparison toward well-studied genes.
- The 'Not detected' normal level (score 0) may reflect true absence or a technical limitation of the IHC assay; it is treated as a valid zero in the scoring scheme.
- Ties in the colon ranking at the 12th position were broken by Ensembl ID ascending; three genes (SLC3A2, NLGN1, SLC13A1, PTPRE, PDPN) share the same change value at the boundary, and the first twelve by Ensembl ID are reported.
