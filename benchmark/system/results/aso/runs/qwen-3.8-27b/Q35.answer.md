**Limitations**

- CPTAC protein log2 fold-change and adjusted p-value are blank (missing) for RAD51, FOXM1, HROB, and C1orf112 in the top-10 table; these blanks are retained explicitly and do not indicate a zero or absent effect.
- Prognostic directions are HPA's own annotations derived from TCGA and validation cohorts as defined by the atlas; they are not independent external validations.
- A direction of 'none' means no prognostic association was reported for that gene in that cohort; it is not evidence that the gene is unassociated with prognosis.
- CPTAC measurements are protein-level (mass-spectrometry-based) and come from a different assay than the RNA-based prognostic annotations; concordance between the two is not implied by their co-presentation.
- The cross-tab statuses do not include a separate 'conflicting' category; genes with both favourable and unfavourable fields nonempty in the same cohort were classified by first-match rule and are not separately enumerated.
