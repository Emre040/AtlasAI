- PROGENy and CytoSig signatures are both inferred from bulk gene-expression profiles; their high mutual correlations reflect shared transcriptional programs captured by overlapping gene sets in the cell-line atlas. Correlation between two inferred signatures does not establish that one pathway causally drives the other. (evidence: a13 row 0: pair_id=TGFb \| TGFB1, r=0.8749 | a37 row 0: R_squared=0.76055)

**Limitations**

- Both PROGENy and CytoSig scores are inferred from the same underlying gene-expression measurements; high inter-signature correlation is partly mechanical (shared input genes) and cannot be interpreted as independent biological validation.
- The OLS fit uses all 1,206 cell lines without stratification by tissue of origin; the slope and R² may be driven by between-tissue variance rather than within-tissue pathway regulation.
- The 'significant' label supplied by HPA was not used as a filter, per the study design; some cell lines carry a 'Non-significant' flag (e.g., KYSE-520 for TGFB1) and are included in the correlation and OLS calculations. (520: not verified against the data of this study)
- The correlation matrix covers 14 PROGENy × 43 CytoSig signatures; signatures with fewer than 30 shared finite-z-score cell lines would be excluded, but all 602 pairs met the threshold in this dataset.
