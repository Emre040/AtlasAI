**Limitations**

- The interaction analysis is limited to the 20 selected genes (top-10 per cohort); interactions with genes outside this set are not captured. The zero-edge result may reflect the biological specificity of surfactant and contractile proteins rather than a true absence of interactions in the full proteome.
- Tissue specificity is based on RNA (nTPM) classification; protein-level tissue enrichment may differ due to post-transcriptional regulation.
- The 'secreted to blood' share uses the HPA 'Plasma proteins' protein-class annotation (experimentally confirmed in blood plasma); it does not capture all proteins that may be present in blood at low abundance.
- Main subcellular location is missing for several genes (7 of 10 lung, 4 of 10 SM), reflecting gaps in the HPA subcellular location annotation.
- The degree bar chart shows all zeros because no edges were found; it is included for completeness but carries no discriminative information.
