**Limitations**

- The interaction analysis is limited to consensus edges recorded in the HPA interaction_consensus table; absence of an edge here does not exclude a physical interaction documented in other databases.
- Subcellular location data for the lung cohort (a5) had rows for only 6 of 17 genes; the 11 missing entries reflect absence from the subcellular_location source table, not a biological absence. (11: not verified against the data of this study)
- The Jaccard index of 0 reflects strict tissue-specificity definitions (RNA tissue specificity = 'Tissue enriched' with the named tissue in RNA tissue specific nTPM); a relaxed definition could yield overlap.
