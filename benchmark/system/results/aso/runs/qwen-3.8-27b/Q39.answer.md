**Limitations**

- The 8 genes (14,862 − 14,854) not appearing in the distinct-gene blood assay counts likely lack a classifiable row in the joined assay table; they are treated as unclassified rather than assigned to 'Neither'.
- Blood concentrations reported are mean values per gene from the HPA blood concentration tables; individual sample-level variability is not shown.
- The 'Not detected' specificity category (n = 378) refers to protein-level detection, not RNA; these genes have detectable RNA in salivary gland but no protein detected by the HPA antibody panel.
- The ranked list is restricted to genes annotated as 'Secreted' in protein class AND detected in blood by at least one assay; genes that are secreted but not yet measured in blood are excluded.
