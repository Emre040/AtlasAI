**Limitations**

- 378 cohort genes carry the 'Not detected' RNA tissue specificity category despite meeting the nTPM ≥ 1 inclusion threshold; this reflects the atlas's specificity classification criteria (which may require detection in a minimum number of samples or a higher expression threshold) differing from the simple nTPM ≥ 1 filter used to define the cohort.
- Olink blood concentrations (mean_conc [pg/L]) are blank for all 12 ranked genes, meaning none of them are measured by the Olink immunoassay panel; their blood concentrations come solely from mass spectrometry.
- The MS blood concentration column records a single value per gene as stored in the atlas; if multiple measurements exist they are not separately reported here.
- The 'secreted to blood' filter used for ranking requires both a 'Predicted secreted proteins' or 'Plasma proteins' protein-class annotation and at least one blood measurement (Olink or MS); genes that are secreted but lack a blood measurement are excluded from the ranking pool.
