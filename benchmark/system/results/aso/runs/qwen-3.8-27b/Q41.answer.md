**Limitations**

- P-values are from the HPA Cox proportional hazards analysis of TCGA survival data and represent statistical association, not causal effect.
- The favourable/unfavourable designation reflects the direction of the hazard ratio in the HPA analysis; a favourable gene is one whose higher expression is associated with better survival.
- Consensus nTPM values are from normal tissue (Kidney and Lung), not from tumour samples, so they do not reflect tumour-specific expression.
- Protein class annotations include predicted categories (e.g. 'Predicted intracellular proteins') which are computational inferences and may not reflect experimentally validated localisation.
- The scatter plot uses the single non-empty prognostic p-value per gene per cancer (any direction); genes with missing p-values in one or both cancers are omitted (381 of 1,066 rows). (381: not verified against the data of this study)
- The gene universe is restricted to Ensembl genes present in the HPA master table; genes not annotated in HPA are excluded.
