**Limitations**

- The IHC data covers only 4 of the 20 genes (YBX1, EGR1, JUND, ELF3); the remaining 16 have no Enhanced or Supported normal-colon IHC record in the atlas, which may reflect lack of annotation rather than true absence of protein in those cell types.
