**Limitations**

- The Human Protein Atlas records relative expression levels (nTPM for RNA, Intensity for MS protein), not absolute molecule counts per cell. No calibration to absolute molecules is available in the atlas, so absolute protein molecules per cell in normal lung cannot be computed from these data.
- CPTAC logFC is a relative fold change. Applying it to a relative MS Intensity baseline yields a relative level, not an absolute molecule count per cell. The atlas does not record absolute protein molecules per cell in tumour.
- A ratio of nTPM to MS Intensity is a ratio of relative levels from different assays (RNA-seq and mass spectrometry), not an absolute protein-to-RNA molecule ratio. The atlas does not measure or record absolute molecule counts of either species per cell.
- Ranking by tumour molecules per cell, the absolute-abundance bar chart, and the molecule-ratio heatmap all require absolute molecule counts that the atlas does not record or can derive from its relative measurements.
