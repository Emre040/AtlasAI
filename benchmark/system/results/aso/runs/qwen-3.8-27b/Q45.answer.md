**Limitations**

- The HPA records prognostic p-values (a statistical association between protein expression level and patient survival) but does not measure the causal effect of pharmacologically inhibiting a gene on survival. A prognostic association cannot be converted into a treatment-benefit estimate: the direction and magnitude of the survival effect of inhibiting a gene are not identified by this data.
- Selecting the best treatment target requires causal evidence of therapeutic benefit from gene inhibition. A prognostic association (protein expression correlated with poor outcome) does not establish that inhibiting that protein would improve survival; the protein may be a marker rather than a driver. Not identifiable from this data.
- No bar chart of predicted survival benefit can be produced because the per-gene survival benefit calculation is not computable from prognostic association data.
