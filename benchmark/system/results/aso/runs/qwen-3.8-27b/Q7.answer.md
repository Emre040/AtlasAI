**Findings**

- Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA) has the most validated prognostic genes of any TCGA cancer cohort, with 1,903 total (1,639 favourable, 264 unfavourable). (evidence: a25 row 3: Cancer=Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), favourable=1639, unfavourable=264, total=1903)
- Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (TCGA) ranks second with 1,052 total (113 favourable, 939 unfavourable); it is the only top-four cancer in which unfavourable genes outnumber favourable ones. (evidence: a25 row 4: Cancer=Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (TCGA), favourable=113, unfavourable=939, total=1052)
- Ovary Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (TCGA) has 17 validated prognostic genes, all unfavourable (0 favourable). (evidence: a25 row 7: Cancer=Ovary Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (TCGA), favourable=0, unfavourable=17, total=17)
- Rectum Adenocarcinoma (TCGA) has 11 validated prognostic genes, all favourable (0 unfavourable). (evidence: a25 row 9: Cancer=Rectum Adenocarcinoma (TCGA), favourable=11, unfavourable=0, total=11)
- The six remaining cancers (Glioblastoma Multiforme, Ovary Serous Cystadenocarcinoma, Rectum Adenocarcinoma, Breast Invasive Carcinoma, Colon Adenocarcinoma, Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma) each have 69 or fewer total validated prognostic genes. (evidence: a25 row 0: Cancer=Breast Invasive Carcinoma (TCGA), total=9; row 1: Cancer=Colon Adenocarcinoma (TCGA), total=8; row 2: Cancer=Glioblastoma Multiforme (TCGA), total=69; row 6: Cancer=Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma (TCGA), total=5; row 7: Cancer=Ovary Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (TCGA), total=17; row 9: Cancer=Rectum Adenocarcinoma (TCGA), total=11)

**Limitations**

- Counts reflect genes with a validated prognostic category in the HPA cancer prognostics table; genes with only unvalidated (training-set) prognostic categories are not included.
- A gene may appear in both the favourable and unfavourable columns for the same cancer if it has been validated in both directions; the total column is the sum of the two and may therefore exceed the number of distinct genes.
- Only TCGA-designated cohorts are shown; validation-study cohorts (e.g. 'Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (validation)') are excluded.
