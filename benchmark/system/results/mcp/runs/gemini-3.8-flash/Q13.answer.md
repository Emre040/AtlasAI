### Cohort Definitions and Characteristics

In the Human Protein Atlas (HPA), **tissue enriched** genes are defined as genes with at least a **4-fold higher mRNA level (nTPM)** in a given tissue compared to the maximum level in any other tissue. 

Because of this strict definition, **a gene cannot be tissue enriched in more than one tissue simultaneously**. Therefore, the pairwise overlap between these cohorts is mathematically zero.
