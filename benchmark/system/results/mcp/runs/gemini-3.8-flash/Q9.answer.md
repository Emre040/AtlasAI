### 1. Definition and Classification of Brain Regional Specificity
In the Human Protein Atlas (HPA) Brain Atlas, regional RNA specificity categories are defined across human brain subregions (including the **amygdala**, **cerebellum**, **cerebral cortex**, **hippocampal formation**, and **hypothalamus**):
- **Region enriched**: Defined as genes with an mRNA level in a particular brain region at least 4-fold higher than in all other analyzed brain regions.
- **Group enriched**: Defined as genes with elevated mRNA levels in a group of 2–4 brain regions at least 4-fold higher compared to any other brain region.
- **Region enhanced**: Defined as genes with mRNA levels at least 4-fold higher in a particular region compared to the average level in all regions.
