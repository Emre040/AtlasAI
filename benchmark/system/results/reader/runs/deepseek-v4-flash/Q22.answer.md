**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

The current release is version 25.1: in the new release version 25.1 of the Human Protein Atlas, a major dataset based on Deep Visual Proteomics has been integrated in the Single cell Resource [1]. The latest addition to the Single Cell resource is Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data, with cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 different cell types representing 14 different tissues [2]. In that experiment, 27 cell types across 14 tissues from a single healthy female donor was analysed using DVP, resulting in detection of almost 14000 proteins across a dynamic range of 8.5 orders of magnitude [3]. In addition, the same release integrated quantitative proteomics profiles (MS-based) for bulk tissue samples into the Tissue resource, with 20 different tissue types overlapping with the DVP samples integrated to the gene-summary pages, providing protein profiles for over 12,700 genes [4]. As for coverage, the twenty-fifth major release of the Human Protein Atlas is described as having over 27,800 antibodies, targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes (~88% of the human protein-coding genes) [5].
