### Notes on the results

- **Liver** stands out for secretion: nearly half its enriched genes (47.6 %) are annotated "Secreted to blood" (plasma proteins such as ALB, HP, SAA1/2, APO*, FGA/B/G, RBP4 dominate the top of the list).
- **Kidney** has the highest favourable-prognosis share (30.2 %); **pancreas** has none (0/62), and its enriched signature is dominated by exocrine digestive enzymes (PNLIP, PRSS1/2, CELA3A/2A, CPA1, CPB1, AMY2A, CTRB1/2) which are among the highest-expression tissue-enriched genes in the entire panel (nTPM up to ~4.2 × 10⁵), giving pancreas by far the largest own-tissue median (2320.8 nTPM).
- Because "Tissue enriched" assigns each gene to a single tissue, all three cohorts are strictly disjoint; the pairwise overlaps are 0 with Fisher p = 1 (Table 2), i.e. no significant co-enrichment is detectable (indeed none is possible under this annotation).

**Caveats**: the figures are rendered here in text (grouped bar chart + log-shaded heatmap) rather than as image files; the exact numeric matrices behind both figures are Tables 1 and the three per-cohort tables above. If a broader notion of "enriched" were intended (e.g. including "Tissue enhanced" or "Group enriched", 558 and 106 genes respectively for these three tissues), the cohort sizes and all downstream shares would change; the numbers above follow the strict HPA "Tissue enriched" definition.
