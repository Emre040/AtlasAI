## 3. Derivation constants

- Total protein molecules per cell = 2×10¹⁰ (standard mammalian cell).
- Normal protein molecules/cell = (gene MS Intensity ÷ Σ lung MS Intensity) × 2×10¹⁰. **Σ lung MS Intensity = 292,670,720,606.1 a.u.** (over 7,146 detected genes).
- Normal mRNA molecules/cell = lung nTPM (convention 1 nTPM ≈ 1 mRNA molecule/cell; 10⁶ mRNA per cell).
- Tumour value = normal value × 2^logFC (both mRNA and protein scaled by the same CPTAC logFC).
- Molecule ratio = protein molecules/cell ÷ mRNA molecules/cell. Because normal and tumour are scaled by the same factor, the ratio is identical in tumour and normal.
