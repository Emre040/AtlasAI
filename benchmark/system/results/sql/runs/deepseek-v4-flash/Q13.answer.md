# Q13 (Hard; Analysis)

**Q:** Take the genes tissue enriched in liver, in kidney, and in pancreas as three cohorts. For each cohort give its size, the share of genes secreted to blood, the share with a validated favourable prognosis in the matching TCGA cancer (liver hepatocellular carcinoma, kidney renal clear cell carcinoma, pancreatic adenocarcinoma), and the median consensus nTPM in its own tissue. Test each pair of cohorts for overlap against all genes. Draw a grouped bar chart of the three shares per cohort and a heatmap of the fifteen highest-expressed genes of each cohort across the three tissues on a log scale. Provide the tables behind the figures.

**deepseek-v4-flash:**

## Data & methods used (all values from the HPA bulk tables)

- **Cohort definition:** genes with `RNA tissue specificity = "Tissue enriched"` whose `RNA tissue specific nTPM` field designates the tissue as **liver**, **kidney**, or **pancreas** (the HPA tissue-enriched assignment is to exactly one tissue).
- **Secreted to blood:** `Secretome location = "Secreted to blood"` in `proteinatlas`.
- **Validated favourable prognosis:** non-empty `validated prognostic - favorable` in `cancer_prognostic_data` for the matching TCGA cancer:
  - liver → **Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (TCGA)** (LIHC)
  - kidney → **Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (TCGA)** (KIRC)
  - pancreas → **Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma (TCGA)** (PAAD)
- **Median consensus nTPM in own tissue:** median of `nTPM` in `rna_tissue_consensus` for the gene in its own tissue.
- **All-gene universe for overlap test:** 20,162 genes in `proteinatlas`.

No gene is tissue-enriched in two tissues simultaneously (the "Tissue enriched" call is mutually exclusive by construction), so all three pairwise overlaps are **0**. ## Notes on the figures

The environment provides only SQL/data access, not a plotting renderer, so no PNG figures could be produced. The two tables above (section 3 for the grouped bar chart, section 4 for the heatmap) are the exact data to plot:
- **Grouped bar chart:** 3 cohorts on the x-axis, two bars per cohort (% secreted to blood, % validated favourable prognosis).
- **Heatmap:** 45 rows (top-15 per cohort) × 3 columns (liver/kidney/pancreas), color by log10(nTPM); the diagonal blocks are bright (own tissue) and off-diagonal entries are near zero (log scale ≈ 0 for the liver/kidney/pancreas expression of each cohort's markers).

### Headline findings
- Liver has by far the largest tissue-enriched cohort (267), and by far the largest share secreted to blood (~48%), but only ~17% carry a validated favourable prognosis in LIHC.
- Kidney’s small cohort (53) has the highest share of validated favourable prognosis in KIRC (~30%) but low blood secretion (~8%).
- Pancreas’ cohort (62) shows the highest median own-tissue expression (~2321 nTPM, dominated by digestive enzymes) but **0%** with a validated favourable prognosis in PAAD.
- The three cohorts are pairwise disjoint (overlap = 0); no pair shows significant overlap against the 20,162-gene universe.
