## Overlap test

The two "lineage enriched" cohorts are **mutually exclusive by construction** (each gene has one assigned specific lineage). Using all 2,197 lineage-enriched genes as the universe:

|  | In B-cohort | Not in B-cohort | Row total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In NK-cohort** | 0 | 65 | 65 |
| **Not in NK-cohort** | 301 | 1,831 | 2,132 |
| **Column total** | 301 | 1,896 | 2,197 |

Observed overlap = 0; expected under independence = 65×301/2197 ≈ 8.9. There is therefore **no positive overlap** — the one-sided enrichment (hypergeometric/Fisher) p-value is 1.0 (the cohorts are non-overlapping by definition; if anything they are mutually exclusive, not co-enriched).
