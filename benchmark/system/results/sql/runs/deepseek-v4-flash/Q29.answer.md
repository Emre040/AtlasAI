## Interpretation caveat
All of the above are **proteomic associations**, not causal effects. The logFC and adjusted p‑values come from observational case–control PEA (proximity-extension assay) plasma protein measurements of each disease relative to Healthy controls. They describe whether a plasma protein's abundance is statistically associated with RA or SLE; they do not establish that any protein drives or protects against disease. The 12 highlighted genes, especially the four discordant (opposing-direction) ones (IRAG2, TRAF2, CRACR2A, MMP7), are candidates for differential regulation between the two autoimmune diseases but require functional/mechanistic studies to test causality.
