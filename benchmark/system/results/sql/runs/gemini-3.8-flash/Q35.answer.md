### 1. Prognostic Direction Replication Assessment

#### Criteria and Status Definitions
- **Favourable**: Non-empty `potential prognostic - favorable` or `validated prognostic - favorable`.
- **Unfavourable**: Non-empty `potential prognostic - unfavorable` or `validated prognostic - unfavorable`.
- **Conflicting**: Non-empty fields in both favourable and unfavourable directions (0 genes across all cohorts).
- **No association reported**: Row present in `cancer_prognostic_data`, but no prognostic fields are populated.
- **Missing cohort row**: Gene present in the HPA master universe (20,162 Ensembl genes in `proteinatlas`) but absent from the specific cohort table.
  - For **Colon Adenocarcinoma**, **Lung Adenocarcinoma**, and **Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma**, exactly 19,973 genes are present in both TCGA and validation cohorts, and 189 genes are missing in both cohorts.
