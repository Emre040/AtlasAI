### 7. Biological Interpretation and Causality Caveats

- **Inferred Nature of Scores:** Both PROGENy and CytoSig scores are computational inferences derived from downstream target gene expression profiles (PROGENy modeling core pathway perturbation; CytoSig modeling cytokine-driven transcriptional footprints).
- **Correlation Does Not Equal Pathway Causality:** A high correlation between an inferred pathway signature (e.g., PROGENy `TGFb`) and a cytokine signature (e.g., CytoSig `TGFB1`, or `JAK-STAT` with `IFNG`/`IFN1`) demonstrates that cell lines exhibiting one transcriptional program frequently manifest the other. However, this does not establish direct upstream-downstream biological causation in every instance.
