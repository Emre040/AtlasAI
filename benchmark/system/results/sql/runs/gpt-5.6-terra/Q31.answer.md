## Design and eligibility

I used the separate `rna_tissue_hpa` and `rna_tissue_gtex` nTPM tables—not the consensus table—for HPA–GTEx comparisons. The universe was restricted to Ensembl genes present in the HPA master (`proteinatlas`) table whose `Protein class` contains **“Transcription factors”**. For each tissue, eligible genes had finite, non-negative nTPM in both sources.

All three tissues had **1,483 eligible transcription-factor genes**. Rankings use descending nTPM and break ties by Ensembl ID. Spearman ranks use average ranks for tied expression values.

## Interpretation and consensus caveat

The separate-source correlations are high across the full eligible TF set (ρ = **0.912–0.948**), with the strongest rank agreement in ovary. The median absolute log-scale differences are modest—about **0.276 to 0.372 log2 units**—but individual TFs can differ by roughly 3–4 log2 units, especially low-to-moderate abundance genes and tissue-regulated factors.

The consensus nTPM file is **not an independent third replication cohort**. It is an HPA-derived integrated/consensus expression product that incorporates the source datasets represented by HPA and GTEx measurements (and may select or aggregate source-derived values). It is therefore statistically dependent on the two compared inputs. Treating it as a third replicate would reuse information from the same underlying sources, inflate apparent replication, and obscure rather than test source-specific disagreement.
