## Method

I used the distinct Ensembl IDs in the HPA master table as the universe (**20,162 genes**). A cohort status was assigned as:

- **F** = favourable: any nonempty *potential* or *validated prognostic—favourable* field
- **U** = unfavourable: analogous unfavourable fields
- **C** = conflicting: both F and U evidence
- **N** = no prognostic association reported: neither directional field populated
- **M** = missing cohort row

“Unprognostic” fields were not used to call an association. No conflicting-status rows occurred in these four cancer pairs.

Same/reversed directions only count F↔F or U↔U, and F↔U/U↔F, respectively. TCGA-only and validation-only include a directional association on that side with the other side N or M.
