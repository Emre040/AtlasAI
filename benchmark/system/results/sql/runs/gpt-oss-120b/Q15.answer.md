import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import seaborn as sns

def plot_top10(df_top, title):
    plt.figure(figsize=(7,5))
    sns.barplot(data=df_top,
                x='log10_ratio',
                y='Gene',
                palette='viridis')
    plt.title(title)
    plt.xlabel('log10 (Immuno‑assay / MS) ratio')
    plt.tight_layout()

## Interpretation

* **Higher median MS concentrations in the liver‑enriched group** – liver‑produced plasma proteins (e.g., albumin, apolipoproteins, coagulation factors) dominate the MS‑detected blood proteome, giving a median ≈ 10⁹.⁹ pg/L versus ≈ 10⁶.⁶ pg/L for the rest.
* **Immuno‑assay vs. MS ratio** – several liver proteins show relatively **higher immuno‑assay** signals compared with MS (e.g., C4BPB, TF), whereas many non‑liver proteins have **much higher immuno‑assay** signals relative to MS (e.g., CRISPLD2, BMP4). This reflects assay‑specific biases (antibody affinity, peptide detectability) and biological secretion patterns.
* **Scatter plot** – the blue liver points are tightly clustered at high MS concentrations, while the orange rest points are more dispersed, confirming that MS preferentially captures abundant liver‑secreted proteins.

These results illustrate the distinct quantitative landscapes captured by immuno‑assays and mass‑spectrometry in human blood, especially for liver‑derived proteins.
