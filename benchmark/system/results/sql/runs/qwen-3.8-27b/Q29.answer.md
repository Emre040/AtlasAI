### Caveat — association ≠ causation
This is an **observational/associational** PEA proteomics measurement of circulating protein levels in patients vs healthy donors. The logFC/p-values describe **correlative protein abundance differences**, not causal effects. Reverse causation, confounding (age, treatment, comorbidities, drug effects — e.g., biologic/DMARD use in RA/SLE cohorts), batch, and the single-shot case-control design all preclude inferring that a changed protein level *causes* the disease. Establishing causation would require longitudinal sampling, targeted intervention, genetic (Mendelian-randomization) evidence, or functional assays. The HPA PEA DE data are best interpreted as **hypothesis-generating proteomic associations.**
