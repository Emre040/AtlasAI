## 4) Derived values — what can and cannot be computed from these tables

**Absolute molecules per cell: not determinable with the tools/data available.** I must say so rather than estimate: the HPA bulk tables contain no calibration that converts lung MS *relative intensity* to copies per cell (no intensity→copy coefficient, no cell number per tissue, no protein mass per cell), and no RNA‑per‑cell constant (total transcripts per cell) to convert nTPM to RNA molecules per cell. No molecules/cell or copy‑number columns exist in any table (verified by column search). The derivations are therefore:

- RNA molecules/cell (normal lung) = nTPM × (transcripts per cell) / 10⁶ — requires the transcripts‑per‑cell constant.
- Protein molecules/cell (normal lung) = MS intensity × (copy‑number per intensity‑unit coefficient).
- Protein molecules/cell (LUAD) = normal × 2^logFC (CPTAC logFC).
- Protein:RNA molecule ratio = (protein mol/cell) / (RNA mol/cell) — same missing factors.
