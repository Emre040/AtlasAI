# Q22 (documentation)

**Q:** What is the current release of the Human Protein Atlas, what new data did it add, and how many antibodies and human genes does the atlas cover in this release?

**deepseek-flash:**

I found the release notes. Here is what the pages show.

## Current release

The most recent release listed on the Human Protein Atlas release-history page is **version 25.1, released 2026.05.25 (Ensembl version 109)** — a minor update. Immediately below it is **version 25.0, released 2025.11.11**, the twenty-fifth *major* release.
(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases)

## New data added

**Version 25.1** (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases):
- **Single Cell:** Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data added — cell-type-resolved, MS-based protein profiles across 27 cell types representing 14 tissues, matched side-by-side with Single Cell Type data (with specificity overlap and agreement scores). Includes 27 cell-type shapes (3–5 replicates each), grouped into 24 cell types.
- **Tissue:** Quantitative MS-based proteomics profiles for bulk tissue samples integrated — 20 tissue types, giving protein profiles for over 12,700 genes.

**Version 25.0** (the major release, https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases) added:
- **Tissue & Cancer:** 3,937 proteins validated with Enhanced strategies; 14 new antibodies.
- **Tissue:** extended profiling expanded with 8 new tissue samples (choroid plexus, efferent ducts, ovary, placental stages, etc.); multiplex tissue profiling updated with a new pancreas panel (77 proteins) and 22 more proteins in ciliated cells (1,106 proteins total across 8 panels); new GTEx blood-vessel RNA-seq.
- **Brain:** updated spatial transcriptomics (3 cerebellum and 3 cerebral cortex samples, stereo-seq).
- **Single Cell:** updated data with 34 tissue types and 154 cell types grouped into 53 groups.
- **Subcellular:** now covers 13,603 genes (77 added, 8 removed) with 15,995 antibodies; new hiPSC assay for 643 genes; SubCell image embeddings.
- **Blood:** expanded with 2,500+ samples across Olink Explore HT and SomaScan 11K (~30 cohorts).
- **Structure:** AlphaFold2 in-house structures for 83,795 isoforms across 19,904 genes.
- **Interaction:** AlphaFold3 structures for 22,979 consensus interactions across 7,855 genes.

## Antibody and gene coverage

The antibody/gene coverage figures are given only in the **version 25.0** notes: "over **27,800 antibodies**, targeting proteins from over **17,400 human genes** (~**88% of the human protein-coding genes**)," across 9 resources.
(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases)

## Note on a gap

The individual version **25.1** notes do **not** state antibody/gene totals — only the 25.0 major-release notes do. So "in this release" is ambiguous: if "current release" means the very latest (25.1), the pages do not give an antibody/gene count for it; the 27,800 antibodies / 17,400 genes figures belong to version 25.0.
