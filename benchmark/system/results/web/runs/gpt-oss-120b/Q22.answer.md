**What new data were added in this release**

- **Cell line:** Protein data from the pan‑cancer project for 542 cell lines were added.  
- **Structure & Interaction:** AlphaFold‑derived 3‑D structures for most human proteins (including isoforms), missense variant data, and integrated interaction networks from IntAct, BioGRID, BioPlex and OpenCell were added.
