**Explanation**

* **Western blot – enhanced methods**  
  The HPA classifies antibody validation results into several “enhanced” categories. For Western‑blot assays the enhanced categories include:  
  • **Capture** – validation using a protein‑capture assay (e.g., protein array).  
  • **Genetic** – validation using genetic knock‑down/knock‑out or CRISPR data.  
  • **Independent** – validation by an independent assay (different laboratory or method).  
  • **Orthogonal** – validation by an orthogonal technique (e.g., comparing to RNA expression).  
  • **Recombinant** – validation using a recombinant‑protein source.  

* **IHC – orthogonal validation**  
  In the IHC workflow, the “orthogonal” validation step checks whether the visual staining pattern of the antibody on tissue sections matches the known RNA expression profile of that gene. This RNA data comes from three large transcriptomics resources (the HPA RNA‑seq dataset, GTEx, and FANTOM). The comparison is performed across the full panel of **44 normal tissues** curated by the HPA, ensuring that the antibody staining is consistent with the expected tissue‑specific expression at the transcript level.
