## Current release: **Version 25.1** (released 2026-05-25, Ensembl version 109)

**Source:** the Human Protein Atlas release-history page (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases), where v25.1 is the top (most recent) entry.

## New data added in v25.1

1. **Single Cell — Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP):** cell-type-resolved, MS-based protein profiles covering **27 different cell types across 14 different tissues**, integrated into the Single Cell resource. 27 cell-type shapes (3–5 replicates each), grouped into 24 cell types for classification, are included. Selected single-cell RNA (Single Cell Type) data are matched to the DVP samples for side-by-side RNA-vs-protein comparison, with specificity-overlap and agreement scores.
2. **Tissue — bulk-tissue mass spectrometry:** quantitative MS-based proteomics profiles for **20 different tissue types** (overlapping with the DVP samples) are integrated into the Tissue resource's gene-summary pages, providing **protein profiles for over 12,700 genes**.

## Antibody and gene coverage

The v25.1 entry itself lists only the two data additions above and gives no updated totals. The current numbers given on the same release page (in the v25.0 entry, 2025-11-11) are: **over 27,800 antibodies, targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes (~88% of the human protein-coding genes)**, across 9 separate resources. Since the v25.1 notes report no changes to the antibody set or gene count, those are the figures for the current release.
