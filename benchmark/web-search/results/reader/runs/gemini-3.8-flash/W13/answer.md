**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply to Western blot?

For Western blot, the Human Protein Atlas applies five enhanced antibody validation methods: Genetic validation, Recombinant expression validation, Independent antibody validation, Orthogonal validation, and Capture MS validation [1].
