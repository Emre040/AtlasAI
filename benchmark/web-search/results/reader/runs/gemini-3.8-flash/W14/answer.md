**Q:** What are the four reliability scores the Human Protein Atlas assigns to annotated tissue protein expression profiles?

The four reliability scores assigned by the Human Protein Atlas to annotated protein expression profiles are Enhanced, Supported, Approved, and Uncertain [1].

Sources:
[1] "a reliability score for each annotated protein expression profile was set as either Enhanced, Supported, Approved, or Uncertain" — The human proteome - Methods summary - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method)
