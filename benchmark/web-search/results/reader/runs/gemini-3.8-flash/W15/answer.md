**Q:** How many cell types and how many human tissues does the Single cell type section of the Human Protein Atlas cover, and how many individual cell type clusters underlie it?

The Single cell type section of the Human Protein Atlas covers 154 individual cell types across 34 human tissues [1]. It is based on single-cell RNA sequencing analysis comprising all protein-coding genes in 1,175 individual cell type clusters [1].

Sources:
[1] "The scRNAseq analysis is based on publicly available genome-wide expression data and comprises all protein-coding genes in 1175 individual cell type clusters corresponding to 15 different cell classes, 53 cell type groups and 154 individual cell types" — The human cell proteome - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell)
