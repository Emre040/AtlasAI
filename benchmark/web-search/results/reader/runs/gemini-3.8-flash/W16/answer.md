# gemini-3.8-flash

**Q:** In the Blood resource, for how many proteins are plasma concentrations taken from immunoassay-based studies, and for how many from mass spectrometry-based studies?

In the Blood resource, plasma concentrations have been gathered from literature immunoassay-based studies for 457 proteins predicted to be secreted in blood [1]. For mass spectrometry-based proteomics studies publicly available in the Peptide Atlas database, estimated plasma concentrations are presented for 4,285 proteins, including both secreted and leakage proteins [1].

Sources:
[1] "Plasma concentrations for 457 proteins predicted to be secreted in blood have been collected from immunoassay-based studies in literature and are displayed together with information on their predicted function. Further, plasma concentrations and functions are presented for 4285 both secreted and leakage proteins, estimated from mass spectrometry-based proteomics studies publicly available in the Peptide Atlas database" — The human blood atlas - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/blood)
