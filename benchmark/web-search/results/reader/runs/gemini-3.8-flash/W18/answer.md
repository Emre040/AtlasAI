# gemini-3.8-flash

**Q:** The cancer section's survival analysis rests on how many TCGA patients and how many cancer types, and from which dated release of the Genomic Data Commons were the samples collected?

The cancer section's survival and RNA expression analysis used cancer patient samples collected from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) project from the initial release of the Genomic Data Commons (GDC) on June 6, 2016 [1]. The analysis rests on RNA-seq data from 21 cancer types, comprising 8,384 samples across the 21 TCGA cancer types and validation cohorts [1][2].

Sources:
[1] "Cancer patient samples used for mRNA expression and survival analysis were collected from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) project from the initial release of Genomic Data Commons (GDC) on June 6, 2016, and information regarding sex, age and other clinical information can be found here. The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) project of Genomic Data Commons (GDC) collects and analyzes multiple human cancer samples. RNA-seq data from 21 cancer types having a corresponding major cancer type with in-house IHC data were included" — The human cancer proteome - Methods summary - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/cancer/method)
[2] "Transcript expression levels summarized per gene in 8384 samples from 21 different TCGA cancer types and validation cohorts corresponding to 10 of the cancer types" — The human cancer proteome in data - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/cancer/data)
