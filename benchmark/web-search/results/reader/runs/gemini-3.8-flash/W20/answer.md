**Q:** Why does the Human Protein Atlas not provide its human tissue RNA-seq data in raw format?

The Human Protein Atlas does not currently provide human tissue RNA-seq data in raw format due to GDPR legislation [1]. However, they are working to make the raw RNA-seq data accessible through a secure federated EGA repository with managed access [2].

Sources:
[1] "Due to GDPR legislation we do currently not provide human tissue RNA-Seq data in raw format." — Help - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/help)
[2] "We are working on making the raw RNA-Seq data available through a secure federated EGA repository where access can be managed." — Help - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/help)
