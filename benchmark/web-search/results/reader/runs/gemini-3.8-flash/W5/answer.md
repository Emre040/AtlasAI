**Q:** In which year was the first version of the Human Protein Atlas website launched, and on roughly how many antibodies was it based?

The first version of the Human Protein Atlas website was launched in 2005 and was based on approximately 700 antibodies (specifically, 718 antibodies in version 1.0) [1][2].

Sources:
[1] "The first version of the Human Protein Atlas website was launched in 2005 and consisted of protein expression data based on approximately 700 antibodies." — history - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/history)
[2] "First release of the Protein Atlas. Atlas content: 718 antibodies and 413,568 images" — Release history - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases)
