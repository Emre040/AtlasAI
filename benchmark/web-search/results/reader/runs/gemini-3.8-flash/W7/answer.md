**Q:** How many antibodies and how many human genes did release 25.0 of the Human Protein Atlas cover?

Release 25.0 of the Human Protein Atlas covered over 27,800 antibodies, which target proteins from over 17,400 human genes (representing approximately 88% of human protein-coding genes) [1].

Sources:
[1] "Twenty-fifth major release of the Human Protein Atlas with over 27,800 antibodies, targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes (~88% of the human protein-coding genes)." — Release history - The Human Protein Atlas (https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/releases)
