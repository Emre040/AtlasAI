**Q:** Which publication does the Human Protein Atlas ask you to cite for its subcellular data? Give the first author, the journal, the year and the PubMed id.

For its subcellular data, the Human Protein Atlas cites the publication "A subcellular map of the human proteome", where the first author is PJ Thul (Thul PJ et al.), published in Science in 2017 with PubMed ID 28495876 [1].

Sources:
