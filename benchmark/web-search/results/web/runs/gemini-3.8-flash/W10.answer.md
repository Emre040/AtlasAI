# W10 (documentation)

**Q:** What nTPM value does the Human Protein Atlas use as the limit of detection, and how much higher must a gene's expression be in one tissue than in any other for the gene to count as tissue enriched?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Human Protein Atlas transcriptomics methods page (https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/transcriptomics):

* **Limit of detection:** The Human Protein Atlas uses a cutoff value of **1 nTPM** as the limit of detection across tissues or cell types (genes with nTPM < 1 in all tissues/cell types are classified as "Not detected"). 
* **Tissue enriched criteria:** A gene's expression level (nTPM) in a particular tissue must be **at least four times (4-fold)** higher than in any other tissue.

*(Source: [Human Protein Atlas – Methods summary: Transcriptomics](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/transcriptomics))*
