**Q:** For immunohistochemistry, what does orthogonal antibody validation compare the staining intensity against, across up to how many normal tissues, and what difference between the two representative tissues is required for the antibody to receive the Enhanced score?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Human Protein Atlas Methods page on [Antibody Validation](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation):

* **What staining intensity is compared against:** It is compared against the **corresponding mRNA levels**.
* **Number of normal tissues:** Across up to **46 normal tissues**.
* **Required difference between the two representative tissues:** A difference in mRNA levels of **at least 4-fold** between the two representative tissues (representing high and low expression of protein and mRNA) is required for the antibody to receive the "Enhanced" validation score.
