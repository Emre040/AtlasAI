**Q:** Which enhanced antibody validation methods does the Human Protein Atlas apply to Western blot?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

According to the Human Protein Atlas (HPA), enhanced antibody validation is adapted from the five pillars proposed by the International Working Group for Antibody Validation (IWGAV). For **Western blot (WB)**, the Human Protein Atlas applies the following five enhanced validation methods:

1. **Genetic validation** (e.g., Genetic validation – siRNA/CRISPR):
   The target protein is knocked down or knocked out in a suitable cell line using genetic tools (such as siRNA or CRISPR). Cell lysates before and after knockdown are analyzed by Western blot, where successful validation requires a significant reduction or absence of the target band (e.g., signal downregulation > 25%).
   *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)*

2. **Recombinant expression validation**:
   The target protein is overexpressed in a cell line that preferably does not express it endogenously. Cell lysates with and without recombinant expression are compared on Western blot, requiring no or weak signal in the unmodified lysate and a strong band in the recombinant expression lysate.
   *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)*

3. **Independent antibody validation**:
   Two independent antibodies targeting non-overlapping epitopes of the same protein are compared across at least two cell lysates (preferably expressing different levels of the target). Both antibodies must yield a similar Western blot banding pattern.
   *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)*

4. **Orthogonal validation**:
   The antibody band intensity is evaluated against target protein levels quantified by an antibody-independent method, such as mass spectrometry (MS), or compared against corresponding RNA levels across at least two cell or tissue samples expressing the target at different levels, showing matching patterns.
   *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)*

5. **Capture MS validation**:
   The molecular weight/migration of the band visualized by the antibody is verified against the protein size and identity determined by mass spectrometry after cutting and analyzing multiple gel slices from the electrophoretic separation.
   *(Source: https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/antibody+validation)*
