**Q:** What are the four reliability scores the Human Protein Atlas assigns to annotated tissue protein expression profiles?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Human Protein Atlas (Methods summary: [Immunohistochemistry - tissues](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue/method/ih+imaging)), each annotated protein expression profile is assigned one of four reliability scores based on an evaluation of immunohistochemical data, RNA-seq data, and available protein/gene characterization literature:

1. **Enhanced** – Assigned when one or several antibodies targeting non-overlapping sequences of the same gene have obtained enhanced validation based on orthogonal or independent antibody validation methods.
2. **Supported** – Assigned when the staining pattern is consistent with RNA levels, valid literature, or paired antibodies targeting non-overlapping sequences (without qualifying for enhanced validation).
3. **Approved** – Assigned when there is partial consistency (e.g., between RNA and protein expression, with literature, or between paired antibodies).
4. **Uncertain** – Assigned when only multi-targeting antibodies are available, when there is low/dissimilar consistency between RNA and staining patterns with no or contradictory literature, or when paired antibodies show dissimilar expression patterns.
