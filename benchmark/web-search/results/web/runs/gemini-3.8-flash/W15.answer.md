**Q:** How many cell types and how many human tissues does the Single cell type section of the Human Protein Atlas cover, and how many individual cell type clusters underlie it?

**gemini-3.8-flash:**

Based on the Single cell type section and its Methods summary in the Human Protein Atlas:

* **Cell types covered:** The section covers **154 different cell types** (which are also summarized into 53 cell type groups for classification and overview). 
  * Source: [The human cell types - The Human Protein Atlas](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type)
* **Human tissues covered:** It covers single-cell/single-nucleus RNA sequencing data from **34 human tissue types** (including blood).
  * Sources: [The human cell types - The Human Protein Atlas](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type) and [The human cell types - Methods summary](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type/method)
* **Underlying individual cell type clusters:** It is underlain by **1,175 individual cell type clusters**.
  * Sources: [The human cell types - The Human Protein Atlas](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type) and [The human cell types - Methods summary](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type/method)
