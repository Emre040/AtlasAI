-
Tissue & Pathology

In total 3942 proteins are validated with Enhanced validation strategies. 5 antibodies were omitted, 69 new antibodies were added.

Tissue

351 proteins underwent multiplex protein profiling, where 192 were analyzed in 6 new cell types/cell states in testis. In total, 742 proteins have been analyzed in 16 cell types in testis and 5 cell types in kidney using multiplex protein profiling, based on overlap of antibody staining with cell/state specific markers.

-
Brain

For this version of the brain section we performed a detailed analysis and comparison of samples. Based clustering analysis we further improved the grouping of samples. We found that the ventromedial nucleus sample, previous group in thalamus, is the ventromedial nucleus of the hypothalamus and is now added to that region. The claustrum which is located between the cortex and basal ganglia was grouped in the basal ganglia, but based on similarities in gene expression is now moved the cerebral cortex group. We also identified that for Nucleus cuneatus some samples were named cuneate nucleus. This has now been corrected and samples are grouped as nucleus cuneatus. When performing our clustering analysis, we found a technical batch effect between RNAseq data based on Illumina and MGI platforms. Correcting for this technical bias affected many of the biological differences between regions of the brain. We therefore decided to separate samples generated on these two sequencing platforms and only normalize samples that have been sequenced using the same method. The consequence of this is that the prefrontal cortex dataset is separated from the rest of the brain samples and expression values of prefrontal cortex areas cannot directly be compared to other regions of the brain. Based on anatomy, function, and protein expression profiles we now organized individual subregions within the regions. For example, subregions in the basal ganglia are now organized as striatum, basal forebrain or globus pallidus. A new feature in the brain section is the UMAP based on distribution of protein coding transcripts in the human brain. This cluster data provides an overview of genes with similar distribution profile that often share functions (e.g. synaptic functions) or are expressed by specific cell types (e.g. oligodendrocytes or immune-cells).

-
Single Cell Type

Single cell RNA data from 1 new tissue (Fallopian tube) was added, all previously published data was revised, and Ovary was replaced by a new dataset. All genes were mapped from Ensembl 103 to Ensembl 109. This resulted in a total of 81 cell types from 31 different human tissues including peripheral mononuclear cells (PBMCs).

-
Subcellular

The subcellular section now contains data for 13147 genes and 15380 antibodies. The two categories for single-cell variations in ICC-IF, previously annotated as spatial- or intensity variations, have now been merged.

-
Cell lines

The Cell line section has been extended. Trancriptomic data of 610 cancer cell lines, including 149 new cancer cell lines from Genentech, as well as 2 cell lines from HPA has been added. In addition, all genes were mapped to Ensembl version 109. This bringing the total count to 1206 unique cell lines representing 28 different cancer types and two non-cancer groups.

- Ensembl transcript biotype protein_coding_LoF were added in this release.

- New HPA sequenced RNA-Seq samples include choroid plexus and ovary.
