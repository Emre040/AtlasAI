Release history

Protein Atlas version 25.1
Release date: 2026.05.25

Ensembl version: 109

-
Single Cell

The latest addition to the Single Cell resource is Deep Visual Proteomics (DVP) data. Cell-type resolved MS-based protein profiles across 27 different cell types, representing 14 different tissues, are integrated into the Single Cell resource. Selected Single Cell Type data are matched to the DVP samples to facilitate a side-by-side comparison of RNA and protein data, accompanied by specificity overlap and agreement scores for systematic RNA-to-protein comparisons. A total of 27 cell type shapes (with 3–5 replicates each), grouped into 24 cell types used for classifications, are included in this release.

- Tissue

Quantitative proteomics profiles (MS-based) for bulk tissue samples are integrated to the Tissue resource. 20 different tissue types, overlapping with the DVP samples are integrated to the gene-summary pages, providing protein profiles for over 12,700 genes.

Protein Atlas version 25.0
Release date: 2025.11.11

Ensembl version: 109

- Twenty-fifth major release of the Human Protein Atlas with over 27,800 antibodies, targeting proteins from over 17,400 human genes (~88% of the human protein-coding genes). 9 separate resources with data covering various aspects of human protein-coding genes.

-
Tissue & Cancer

In total 3,937 proteins are validated with Enhanced validation strategies. 14 new antibodies were added. The standard IHC profiling of one antibody was updated and all tissue & cancer images were replaced with new data resulting from restaining of the antibody.

-
Tissue

The Tissue resource has expanded the extended protein profiling with eight new tissue samples, including e.g. choroid plexus, efferent ducts, ovary and different phases of placental development. Additional 277 proteins were validated with IHC on extended tissue samples. In total, 873 proteins have been validated on 29 different extended tissue samples.

Multiplex tissue profiling has been updated with a new pancreas antibody panel used to study 77 proteins. Additional 22 proteins were studied in ciliated cells. In total, 1106 proteins have been analyzed using 8 antibody panels.

New GTEx RNA-seq samples from blood vessels have been added to the consensus bulk RNA data.

-
Brain

Spatial transcriptomics data is now updated. Now 3 cerebellum section and 3 cerebral cortex samples (stereo-seq platform) are added.

For each sample a crop including all tissue layers is included and the transcript counts for the main cell types (neurons/Purkinje cells, astrocytes, oligodendrocytes, microglia and vascular cells) are calculated for each layer for each sample.

-
Single Cell

The updated single cell type data represents 34 different tissue types, from 35 separate data sources. Brain cells are now represented by a subsampling of the brain single nuclei data (Siletti. K, et al.), separated into neuronal and non-neuronal (two cell UMAPs from the same data source). Eye is represented by a dataset of general eye-related cells, as well as a retina sample with only retinal cells (one tissue type but two separate sources and UMAPs). Other changes include: Adrenal gland, pituitary gland (single nuclei), epididymis and urinary bladder are newly added tissues, bronchus is removed, the data source is changed for PMBC/blood, bone marrow, esophagus, lymph node, pancreas, spleen, stomach. Single nuclei data is added to replace previous single cell data for adipose tissue, heart muscle, skeletal muscle and kidney.

The updated data includes 154 different cell types representing the whole body, these are grouped into 53 main cell type groups for a better overview and categorization. Cell type specificity is calculated for both 154 cell types and the 53 cell type groups. The 15 color-coded cell icons, previously referred to as cell groups, are now called cell class.
